### Best pitches (pitches report, 15 verified small-account cases, 1.3M pitch-email dataset, SEMA 2027 rules)
63. **There is no independent-builder door at SEMA.** Every vehicle must be owned or sponsored by an exhibiting company; feature-vehicle space is $499 to $599, applied for by the sponsor by about July 30, 2027 and paid by August 6; the 2027 display rules cap non-exhibitor logos on the truck at two marks of 50 square inches and allow no signage for non-exhibiting tire brands. Partners who exhibit are worth more, and the hand-lettering plan should favor them. Show spot first, sponsors second, is the repeating pattern (Project 15 Motorsports, 2,140 YouTube subs, a 1968 F-250 on a 2007 Super Duty chassis, the closest Rival analog, had its SEMA entry paid by Baja Designs).
64. **Small accounts get real parts when the brand can use the footage, never for follower count:** a 274-follower account got a Derale kit and a Collab credit; a 3,987-follower shop became Power Hungry's launch venue. Sixteen diesel and truck programs have no follower floor; KC Turbos and KC HiLiTES have floors; Hot Shot's Secret, Nitto and Toyo Treadpass are invite-only; Industrial Injection's sponsorship page is dead, so the text to Scott Dana is the application. Harbor Freight's June 28 invitation is still unanswered.
65. **The pitch that gets answered:** a specific first line about the brand, a link, one content idea, no rates, no call request, a yes-or-no close, under 120 words, "Collab" in the subject (10.6 percent replies vs 2.1 for "Sponsorship"), sent to the partnerships person by name, one follow-up at day 3 or 4. Code-only counter: take the code as the yardstick, ask for one part, one episode, one Collab, then the dealer-cost fallback. DM, email and PRI templates in Jacob's voice are in the report.
