ENGLISH GLOSS:
Forget VN and CapCut. You can now do slow-fast speed ramping inside the Edits app itself. Open Edits and add your video. Select the clip, go to Speed, tap Curve, then go to Custom. You will see a few points. Move the slider between the points and add one more point between each pair. Wherever you pull a point down the video runs slow; wherever you push it up it runs fast. Leave the first point alone, pull the second point down, push the next one up a little, then alternate the rest up and down. Leave the last point at normal. Your slow-fast edit is ready. (Note: speed curves with preset and custom options shipped in the Edits mobile app on 2026-11-12 per Social Media Today; this tutorial from 10-28 shows it was already live for some users.)
