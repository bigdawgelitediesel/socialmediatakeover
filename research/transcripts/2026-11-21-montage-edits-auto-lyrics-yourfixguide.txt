How to add auto lyrics in your Instagram
Reels. So, this is useful if you want to
add automatic captions to your edits in
Instagram. Now, the first thing you need
to do is go and download Instagram's
edits app. Now, you can do this by
opening up the App Store on your phone.
From the search box, go and search for
edits, and then you'll need to click on
this app, which is edits video editor,
and this is Instagram's official video
editor app. So, just go and install this
onto your iPhone, and then you can open
up the app. Once the app's open, you'll
need to sign in to your Instagram
account, and then you can go and create
your first project. So, click on the
plus icon in the bottom right-hand side,
and then go and select gallery. Now,
select the video that you want to edit
and add the captions for, and then click
on the tick icon to go and add this to
your timeline. Now, in order to add
automatic lyrics to this video, make
sure the video is deselected like this.
You can then click on captions at the
bottom of the page. You'll have some
customization options. So, for example,
you can click on styles, and here's
where you can go and customize the
caption font style. So, you can change
things like the color and all of the
different styles from here. You can also
click on transcription controls, and
this is where you can filter certain
words, and you can also choose to turn
on the punctuation as well. Now, once
you've done this, just press generate
captions, and this will generate the
automatic captions for this video. Now,
once the captions have been generated,
you can go and play through your
footage. You can customize any of these
captions. So, you can go and make these
shorter or longer depending on your
preference. You can also edit any of the
text inside of these caption boxes. Now,
once you're happy, just press next in
the top right-hand side, and this will
begin exporting your video. You can then
either share this directly to the
Instagram app and upload this as a reel,
or you can press the download button,
and you can go and save this to your
phone's gallery and you can go and
upload it directly in the Instagram app.
