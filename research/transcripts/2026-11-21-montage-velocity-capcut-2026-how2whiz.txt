Want to create smooth velocity edits
like viral TikTok and anime edits?
In this video, I'll show you how to make
amazing velocity edits using CapCut
step-by-step. Let's get started. First,
open CapCut and create a new project.
Import the video clips you want to edit.
Choose clips with fast movement, action
scenes, dance clips, sports moments.
Velocity edits work best with motion.
Cut unnecessary parts from the video.
Keep only the best moments. Shorter
clips create smoother and more energetic
edits.
Tap on your clip, then select speed and
tap curve. You'll see several presets
like montage hero, bullet flash in.
Choose one to start. For advanced edits,
tap custom. Now add speed points
manually. Example,
slow down before the beat, speed up
during transitions, slow motion on
impact moments. This creates the famous
velocity effect. Import your music
track. Use beat markers to match speed
changes with the song.
Velocity edits look much better when
synced perfectly to music. Now enhance
your edit with effects like
motion blur, shake, flash blur
transitions. These make the edit feel
smoother and more cinematic. Now adjust
contrast, saturation, sharpening. This
gives your edit a professional anime or
cinematic look. To avoid laggy slow
motion, use high FPS clips if possible.
Enable smooth slow motion options. This
improves edit quality dramatically.
Don't overuse speed changes. Good
velocity edits are smooth, beat synced,
easy to follow. Too many effects can
make the video messy.
And that's how to make velocity edits in
CapCut.
