Everyone is fighting over visual hooks, but no one is using audio ones. Here are five you can use in your next video. One is the sound first open. You hear before you see it. A shutter, a pour, or a zipper. The ears actually hook before the eye. Two is the mid-sentence start. Cut in halfway through a thought, your brain wants to process the beginning. Three is the silence drop. Kill everything for half a second before the key line. Four the room change. Clean studio voice into raw audio off the camera. That texture shift basically shows something has changed or happened. Five is the off-screen voice. "Jason, is that really all it takes? Because honestly, that seems way too easy." Damn.
