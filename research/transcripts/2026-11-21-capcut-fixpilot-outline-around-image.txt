Want a clean white outline around an image in CapCut PC? It helps thumbnails, product photos, and cutouts
stand out. Open CapCut PC and import your image. Drag it down onto the timeline. The effects are applied to
the clip from there. Click the image clip once. The editing menu appears in the top right corner. Open the
remove BG tab and turn on auto remove. This cuts out the background so the outline shows clearly around your
subject. If the background is busy, check that the subject is picked correctly before moving on. Stay in that
menu and click stroke. Set the color to white. White gives a bright, high contrast look, especially on dark
backgrounds. Now use the thickness slider. Keep it thin for a clean, simple look. Make it thicker if you want
the image to pop like on a thumbnail. Preview it on the canvas. If the outline looks off, adjust the thickness
until it fits the image. If this helped, like the video and subscribe to Fix Pilot for more easy CapCut
tutorials. See you in the next one.
