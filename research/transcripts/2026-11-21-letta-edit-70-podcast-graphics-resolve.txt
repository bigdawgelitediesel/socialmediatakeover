This is every preset inside Let's Edit Podcast, 70 of them for video podcasts and the clips you cut from them in DaVinci Resolve. I'll go folder by folder starting with the multi-camera layouts. The 20 layouts are effects. Each one places one clip into one cell. Put every camera on its own track. Drop the same layout on each and pick its cell in the inspector. Two up split is two cameras side by side rounded is the same with rounded cards. Two up host big gives the host the bigger cell. Three up panel puts three cameras in a row. Host top puts the host on top and two guests below. Four up grid is four cameras in a grid. Picture in picture puts a second camera in the corner. Guest window is a full shot with a guest window. Remote call grid is for remote guests and side card leaves room beside the speaker for graphics. The vertical layouts are for clips. Stacked two cam puts one camera above the other. Stacked two cam rounded does it with rounded cards and stacked three cams stacks three. Split reaction is for a reaction under a clip. Top cam plus caption space leaves the lower half for captions. Vertical PIP, vertical single framed, speaker card clip frame shorts and square clip frame, a single speaker for shorts and square posts. Folder two, name plates. 15 of them. Host plate, guest plate and guest plate plus title name the people on the show. Expert plate adds a topic tag. Lower third clean is a light card. Minimal name is just the name. Name box bold is a bold color box. Name underline underlines the name. Pill plate is a rounded pill. Sidebar plate has a colored split plate splits the name and the title. Social handle plate shows a handle. Remote guest tag marks a remote guest. Speaker switch highlight shows who's talking and two speakers names two people at once. Folder three, quotes. 10 pull quotes. Big quote serif pull quote card and quote plus speaker. Put the line on a card with who said it. Quote highlight highlights the key phrase. Hot take card is for the spicy one. Stat quote is for a number worth repeating. Quote bubble is a speech bubble. Quote typewriter is a dark card version. Quote marks minimal is the words between quote marks and quote vertical is laid out for vertical clips. Folder four, plugs and badges. 10 small on-screen markers. Live badge shows live in the watching count. On air bug, rec. Episode bug and logo corner placeholder set in the corners. Timestamp bug shows the time and the topic. Topic ticker runs topics along the bottom. Subscribe corner is a small follow button. Clip hook header puts the hook at the top of a clip. And simulated waveform is a decorative waveform. It doesn't react to audio and it says so in the name. Folder five, episode cards. 10 of them. Show intro card, season card, episode card and episode number. Big open the show and the episode. And episode title typewriter types the title. Guest intro card introduces today's guest. Coming up lists what's ahead with timestamps. Chapter topic marks a new topic. Previously on the show recaps the last episode. And outro card says thanks for listening. And five transitions for switching cameras. Cam switch slide, cut flash and soft push. Plus mic swipe and waveform wipe. Every title has the same in spector in and out animations. Every text, the light glass or dark theme and a drop shadow. The layouts add zoom, offset, a highlight for the active speaker and name tags. The 16 by 9 layouts are made for wide timelines and the vertical ones for vertical timelines. It was tested in DaVinci Resolve Studio 21 on a Mac. That's Let a Podcast. The link is in the description.
