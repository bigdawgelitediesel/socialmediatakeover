Want to put text behind anything, like this? It takes about a minute in CapCut. Start a new project, open the
library, and search for a clip with one clear subject. Drag it onto the timeline. Move the playhead to 5
seconds, hit control B to split, and delete the rest. Now go to text and drag default text onto the timeline.
Type your word, I'm using wild, set the size to 60, and make it bold. Stretch the text so it covers the whole
clip. Now copy and paste your video. Here's the key step. Drag that copy above the text layer. Select the top
copy, go to remove BG, and tick auto removal. And now your subject walks right in front of the text. That's
text behind subject. Subscribe for a new CapCut trick every week.
