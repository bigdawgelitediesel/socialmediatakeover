Two different decisions. Mistake number two is thinking saturation is just one global slider. DaVinci Resolve's normal saturation control is useful. So, instead of only asking how much saturation do I want, sometimes I ask which saturation levels do I want to affect. One method is to switch a dedicated node to HSV and leave only channel two saturation active. Gain gives me a predictable overall saturation increase. Gamma lets me reshape more of the middle of the saturation range, so moderately saturated colors can gain life without pushing the strongest colors as aggressively.
