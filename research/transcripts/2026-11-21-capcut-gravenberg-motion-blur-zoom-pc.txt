This tutorial shows you how to add motion blur to a zoom by rendering the zoom movement first in CapCut
desktop. Start with a separate project containing the shot you want to zoom. Select the video and open video
basic in the right panel. Move to the first frame and add a scale keyframe using the diamond button. Keep the
starting scale at 100%. Move forward about half a second, add another keyframe, and increase scale to 130%.
Play the shot to check the zoom speed. Move the second keyframe closer to the first for a quicker zoom or
farther away for a slower one. Export this short zoom pass at the same resolution and frame rate you intend to
use in the edit. Then import the exported file into your main project and place it where the zoom belongs.
Select the imported zoom video, open video basic, and find motion blur. And enable it. Adjust the blur and
blend controls available in your build, and click apply if prompted. Let the processing finish before
comparing the result. Start with a moderate amount and pause during the moving section to inspect the blur.
The useful detail is that the zoom is now part of the imported video's frames. This workflow gives the
processing tool moving imagery to analyze rather than relying on it to include live scale keyframes. Check the
settled frame after the zoom, too. Reduce the settings if the picture keeps an excessive ghosted trail.
