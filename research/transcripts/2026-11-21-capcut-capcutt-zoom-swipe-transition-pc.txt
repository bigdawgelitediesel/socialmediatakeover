Want your clips to flow like this? Here's how to add a smooth transition in CapCut. Start a new project. Open
the library and search for your first clip. I'm using snowy mountains. Download it and drag it onto the
timeline. Move the play head to 4 seconds, hit control B, and delete the rest. Short clips make transitions
feel snappier. Now search for a second clip. Pick something that contrasts, like this desert. Drag it onto the
timeline right after the first clip. Open transitions and search zoom. Zoom swipe is free and looks great.
Drag it right onto the cut between your two clips. If CapCut asks about duplicate frames, just hit okay. The
default is 1 second. I'm dropping it to 0.6 for a snappier swipe. Play it back and there it is. Snow to desert
in one swipe. Subscribe for a new CapCut trick every week.
