# Cross-posting decision: should @elcoyotediesel mirror its reels to TikTok, Shorts, Facebook, Threads and Snapchat (Nov 2026)

Research agent sweep run 2026-11-21 for @elcoyotediesel (577 Instagram followers, Creator account, Project Rival, 2 to 4 reels a day, memes share, build reels do not). Question: mirror the same reels to other platforms this month, and if so how, without adding work.

Read first: `reel-references/2026-11-21-diesel-niche-winners/BREAKDOWN.md` section 2 item 8 (same clip, Dirty Diesel Customs 75.8k on TikTok vs 367 on Shorts; TikTok is where diesel shop clips run; JMR wins on Shorts only with cold starts) and `2026-11-21-live-instagram-algorithm.md` (Mosseri Oct 30, 2026: "we don't demote content for being created with other apps," but a visible third-party logo does get less distribution). This file adds the other platforms' side of the ledger.

Sourcing note: TikTok's own help pages (Creator Rewards, For You feed standards), TikTok profile pages and YouTube channel pages all returned empty shells to the fetch tool, so TikTok program numbers below are third-party consensus and are labelled that way. Pew, Buffer, Creafico, vidIQ, Socialinsider, Tubefilter, YouTube Help, Snapchat Help and the Buffer/Metricool pricing pages were read directly. Every number carries a date and a URL.

---

## 0. The short answer

Yes to TikTok and YouTube Shorts, with the same master file and a one-line caption change each. Yes to the Facebook toggle (one switch, zero work per post). No to Threads video, no to Snapchat Spotlight. Tool: Buffer Free (3 channels: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube), master exported straight from the ffmpeg pipeline with no app logo. Cost: about 5 to 10 minutes a day on top of what Instagram already costs. Measure for 30 days, then keep or cut per platform with the kill rules in section 8.

The one real caveat: nothing in the data says TikTok or Shorts will move the needle for a 577-follower account in 30 days. What the data does say is that the marginal cost is close to zero once the file is clean and the scheduler is set up, that TikTok is where the diesel niche's short clips actually travel, and that Shorts is the only one of the three with search and a long tail. That is enough to run the test; it is not enough to change what gets shot.

## 2. YouTube Shorts, Nov 2026

**Shorts series.** Announced Sep 23, 2026 at Made on YouTube: group Shorts into seasons and episodes, custom thumbnails, sequential playback, across mobile, web and TV; secondary coverage says it is rolling out to Partner Program creators, and there was no Help Center page at first. YouTube's number behind it: 40 percent of microdrama views and 30 percent of microdrama clicks came more than 30 days after upload, microdramas passed 6.5B views in H1 2026, watch time up 50 percent year over year. On Oct 27, 2026 YouTube added memberships to series (free episodes, then members-only). (Netinfluencer Oct 28, 2026, https://www.netinfluencer.com/youtube-launches-shorts-series-feature-as-microdramas-pass-6-5b-views-in-h1-2026/; MediaPost, https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/418231/youtube-rolls-out-feature-to-encourage-episodic-co.html and https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/419074/youtube-rolls-out-shorts-series-subscriptions.html; Social Samosa, https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/youtube-shorts-series-feature-for-serialised-storytelling-12760212)

Correction to the brief: the "40 percent of views after day 30" figure is YouTube's microdrama/series stat, not a general Shorts stat. For standalone Shorts the evidence points the other way, see next item.

**Long tail and the day-30 behaviour.** Three datasets, none official:
- vidIQ, 18,423 videos: Shorts reached 95 percent of their day-30 views by day 7 (cited in the search summary; vidIQ research hub, https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-first-10-videos-study/).
- Socialinsider, 2025 data, brands 1k to 1M followers: view buckets 833 (days 1 to 5), 1,786 (5 to 10), 150, 555, 500, then 3,310 in days 25 to 30, which they describe as "their biggest spike nearly a month after they go live." Method is thin, and nothing is reported past day 30. (https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/how-long-does-it-take-for-yt-shorts-to-get-views/)
- Mario Joos's "flattening": on channels doing 100M to 1B monthly views, impressions on Shorts older than 28 to 30 days fell sharply from mid-September 2025; YouTube never confirmed it. (Search Engine Journal, Dec 5, 2025, https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-shorts-algorithm-may-now-favor-fresh-over-evergreen/562661/)
Todd Sherman's standing line: early views are exploratory; a Short that does not beat the feed stops there "though it may blow up another day" (YouTube Blog Q&A, https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/renes-top-five-march-4-2024/). Net: Shorts are front-loaded like everywhere else, with an occasional second push; the long tail that matters is search (a "7.3 Powerstroke cold start" title keeps getting found), not the feed.

**New channels.** vidIQ's first-10-videos study (448,413 channels created Jan to Jun 2025, published Jul 28, 2026): median first Short, 1,362 views at day 30; median first long-form video, 180. (https://vidiq.com/research/youtube-first-10-videos-study/) Buffer's 2025 median for a YouTube Short published through Buffer was about 268 views, up from 86 in 2024 (Buffer, Mar 5, 2026, https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/). Note YouTube counts a Short view on start or replay since Mar 31, 2025, so numbers are not comparable to Instagram's.

**Originality update, Oct 1, 2026.** TeamYouTube Help Community: Shorts recommendations will favour original clips and show less from channels that "primarily aggregate or re-upload other creators' videos without significant changes." Judged at channel level, recommendations only, no threshold published. It targets re-uploads of other people's work; your own clip cross-posted is not the target. (Relevant Audience, https://www.relevantaudience.com/youtube/youtube-shorts-original-content-reach-update/; Noticemesenpai, https://noticemesenpai.com/news/youtube-shorts-reuploads-reduced-reach-cross-posting-audit/)

**Monetization thresholds.** Ad revenue tier: 1,000 subscribers plus 10M qualified Shorts views in 90 days or 4,000 watch hours in 12 months (YouTube Help, https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851). Fan funding tier: 500 subscribers, 3 public uploads in 90 days, 3M Shorts views or 3,000 hours. From Feb 1, 2027, new applicants need 20M Shorts views in 90 days or 8,000 hours, and Shorts ads require 10M Shorts views in 90 days for everyone; existing partners are grandfathered. (Tubefilter, Aug 10, 2026, https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/08/10/youtube-partner-program-ad-eligibility-requirements-shorts/) Shorts money is not the reason to be there; a 500-subscriber fan-funding channel by SEMA 2027 is a realistic side goal, and it unlocks Shopping.

**Diesel/automotive Shorts for small channels.** No dataset breaks this out. The only in-niche evidence is the BREAKDOWN: JMR Rebuilders (small channel, weekly long-form on Fridays, no TikTok found) wins on Shorts only with cold starts and "super rare diesel coldstart" style titles; Industrial Injection's engine-build Shorts do about 1,300; DDC's install Shorts about 300. Searchable noun titles plus real engine audio is the Shorts pattern; build process is not.

## 4. Watermarks, fingerprints, and the clean way to mirror

What each platform actually says, as of Nov 2026:
- **Instagram.** Mosseri, Oct 30, 2026 Stories AMA: no demotion for being made in another app; a visible third-party logo does get less distribution. The originality page lists "has no visible watermarks" among recommendation requirements; your own logo is fine. (algorithm file; The Next Web background, https://thenextweb.com/news/instagram-wont-recommend-reels-with-a-tiktok-logo-and-its-unfair) The Trial Reels note in the algorithm file (stuck at 0 is often "watermarked") is the same rule.
- **TikTok.** Community Guidelines keep "reused or unoriginal content posted without creative edits, such as clips that show someone else's watermark or logo" out of the For You feed (quoted by Post-Bridge, https://www.post-bridge.com/blog/does-cross-posting-hurt-engagement; the TikTok page itself returned only its index to the fetch tool). An Instagram-branded download of your own reel qualifies as "watermark."
- **YouTube.** The Oct 1, 2026 originality update targets re-uploads of other creators' work. No published YouTube statement names competitor watermarks; blogs claiming "YouTube confirmed" could not be traced to a YouTube source. Treat as: don't, but the penalty size is unknown.
- **Facebook.** Monetization policy guides list "a TikTok or another app logo burned into the frame" as an unoriginal-content flag (vendor guides, Fluxnote); reach effect unpublished.
- **Detection.** No platform publishes how it spots watermarks; everything about "fingerprint classifiers" is speculation. Metadata is stripped on upload by all three; the visible pixels are what matter.

**The clean way.** The master already comes out of `tools/build_reel2.py` / `build_reel3.py` as 1080x1920 H.264 with no app logo; the platform-delivery file (`2026-11-20-editing-platform-delivery.md`) already specifies the TikTok and Shorts encodes (same filter chain, 30 fps, SDR BT.709, TikTok `-crf 18 -maxrate 12M`, Shorts `-crf 16 -maxrate 20M`, 1080p cap, 3 min cap) and the combined safe zone (top 290, bottom 680, left 120, right 200). Rules:
1. Never download a reel back from Instagram or TikTok to re-upload. Upload the pipeline master to every platform.
2. CapCut finishes: check the export has no CapCut outro clip and no template watermark before it leaves Jacob's phone; if a reel was finished in CapCut, that export is the master for all three.
3. Keep text inside the combined safe zone so TikTok's taller right rail and Shorts' subscribe chip do not cover captions.
4. Original audio only on auto-publish. A reel built on a licensed trend sound cannot be auto-published through any API (Buffer: "we do not have access to TikTok's audio/music library"); post those natively on Instagram and let Buffer send a phone notification for TikTok. This fits the house rule that the song gets added inside Instagram: the pipeline master (engine audio, no song) is what Buffer sends to TikTok and Shorts, and the Instagram version with the song is never downloaded or mirrored.

**Tools and 2026 limits (pricing pages read directly).**
- Buffer Free: 3 channels, 10 scheduled posts per channel at a time, "refill anytime" (slot reopens when a post publishes), no monthly cap, 30-day analytics, auto-publish to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram creator/business, Facebook Pages, Threads, Bluesky. Next tier $5 per channel per month billed yearly. (https://buffer.com/pricing; Buffer Help, https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/how-many-posts-can-i-schedule-in-advance-Kmy2IEecqm) TikTok via Buffer: one video, 3 s to 10 min, 1 GB, MOV/MP4/WEBM, 2,200-character caption, 5 hashtags, publishes public by default, Business account not required, Promote does not work on Buffer-sent posts (https://support.buffer.com/en-us/articles/using-tiktok-with-buffer-oGEroY9Of2).
- Metricool Free: 1 brand, 20 published posts per month (drafts do not count; a reel sent to 3 networks uses 3), 30-day analytics, all networks except LinkedIn and X; Starter from $20 to $25 per month (https://metricool.com/pricing/; Metricool TikTok help: auto-publish for personal and business accounts, MP4 up to 500 MB, 3 s to 10 min, https://help.metricool.com/en/article/schedule-and-post-on-tiktok-pl1qpr/). At 60 to 120 reels a month the free cap is gone in a week. Buffer Free is the fit.
- Native fallback, zero tools: TikTok Studio on desktop schedules 15 minutes to 10 days ahead (web only); YouTube Studio schedules indefinitely; Instagram schedules up to 25 posts a day about 30 days ahead; Meta Business Suite covers Facebook. (BulkPublish, https://www.bulkpublish.com/blog/can-you-schedule-tiktok-posts/; Donivo, https://donivo.app/blog/social-media-platforms-native-scheduling)

## 7. Recommendation

**Platforms.** Mirror to TikTok and YouTube Shorts now. Turn on "Recommend on Facebook" for all reels (one switch). Skip Threads video and Snapchat Spotlight entirely.

**Which reels.**
- Shorts gets everything (no frequency penalty found, and a channel needs a library for search to work), but the only reels worth a custom title are sound clips, cold starts, walkarounds, and partner-part reveals: nouns people search ("1959 Ford F600 7.3 Powerstroke cold start", "triple-disc 4R100 first drive"). Build-process reels go up with the Instagram caption as the title and no further effort.
- TikTok gets 1 to 2 a day, not 4: the meme, the tribe-news clip, the face-in-corner news reel, the 60 s plus sound clip. Creafico's data says 3 plus a day on a cold account cuts per-video reach 89 percent for the same weekly total; Instagram can absorb the volume, TikTok should get the best two. Open it as a personal/creator account so Creator Rewards stays available at 10k.
- Facebook gets whatever Instagram posts; cost is zero, and the Pew numbers (rural 76 percent, men 50 to 64 at 71 percent) make it the one surface where the truck-owning customer actually is.

**What to change per platform (and only this).**
- TikTok: first caption line is the hook, 3 to 5 hashtags (#dieseltruck #7.3powerstroke #cartok plus one build tag), original audio, keep text inside the combined safe zone. No re-edit.
- Shorts: title is a searchable noun phrase, not a joke; one-line description with "Project Rival, 1959 Ford F600, Road to SEMA 2027" and the site; group into a playlist named for the build (Shorts series proper needs YPP by all accounts, so a playlist stands in until 500 subs).
- Instagram: unchanged.
- Facebook: unchanged; check Insights shows Facebook reach per reel after the first week; if reels post as a still image (known 2026 bug), re-enable the share type in Accounts Center.

**Tool.** Buffer Free, three channels (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube). Master from the ffmpeg pipeline, never a platform download. Reels built on licensed audio: native on Instagram, TikTok via Buffer's Notify Me.

**What this does not change.** Nothing here argues for shooting differently. The BREAKDOWN rules (tribe-named news, hater quote on screen, sound as a format, raw at milestones, caption as hook) hold on all three. Platform fit decides where the clip goes and what the first line says.

## 8. What to measure after 30 days (through Dec 21, 2026)
