## 3. Workflows as numbered procedures

**W1. The 48-hour read (every reel)** (playbook/09:5 to 18; memory:37, 39)
1. At 48 h run `INSTAGRAM_GET_IG_MEDIA_INSIGHTS` with `metric: ["views","reach","saved","likes","comments","shares","total_interactions","ig_reels_avg_watch_time","reels_skip_rate"]` on the media id (Harley Trade `18146810329717837`; find the Dougie reel by caption or timestamp).
2. Compute sends per reach = `shares / reach`; watch share = `ig_reels_avg_watch_time / 1000 / duration`; likes per reach = `likes / reach`.
3. Grade against the table: sends floor 1.0 percent / goal 2.0; skip under 45 / 35; watch 50 / 60 percent; likes 3 / 6 percent; saves and comments as secondary.
4. Apply the fix per miss (low sends = change who it is for and caption line 1; high skip = first 2 s open on a person or a move; low watch = re-hook at 9 to 10 s or cut length; high saves low sends = wrap the tip in a tribe or argument frame).
5. Compare with the meme baseline (1 to 5k views, skip under 40, sends over 1 percent) and the build baseline (200 to 500 views, 0 shares).
6. Log in the reel's project folder README and in `data/instagram/posts.tsv`.
7. Do not call a reel settled before about 72 h; 24 h numbers understate 2 to 3x (README:325 to 327).

**W2. The 20-post pull** (playbook/09:28 to 38; template analysis/2026-11-21-live-pull-20-posts.md)
1. One call: `INSTAGRAM_GET_IG_USER_MEDIA` with `ig_user_id: "me", limit: 20, fields: "id,caption,media_type,media_product_type,permalink,timestamp,like_count,comments_count,insights.metric(views,reach,saved,shares,total_interactions,ig_reels_avg_watch_time,reels_skip_rate)"`. Never request `reposts` or `profile_visits` on reels.
2. In the workbench, per post: `m = {d.name: d.values[0].value for d in post.insights.data}`.
3. Build the table: posted (UTC), type, caption, likes, cmts, views, reach, shares, saves, avg watch s, skip percent.
4. Write the "what the numbers say" list. Memory:38: ALWAYS pull the last 12 posts before picking footage; he posts 2 to 4 times a day.
