## 2. The never list (deduplicated, with sources)

On screen and in the edit
- No on-screen call to action, no spoken "tag him", no burned "send this to", no handle card, no pointing at camera (Jacob; AD 2; SE s3; CLAUDE.md).
- No outro, no end card, no fade to black, no hold after the payoff; cut inside 1.5 s (MP s2; SK; SE rule 2).
- Never "tag 3 friends", "comment YES", "double tap if", "follow before this gets deleted", giveaway-for-follow (MP s2; 09-15 craft playbook s3; CG s2e).
- Never a wide, a logo, or "hey guys" on frame 1; never a static face until 2.7 s; never a product on a clean table as frame one (MP s2; AD 20, 43; BS 5.5).
- Nothing else burned in beside the one lockup on the fast tier; no location pills, no caption band, no per-word highlighted captions, no outlined or stroked text (FL; SK; AD 46).
- Rejected type: Oswald Bold + Montserrat caption system, Anton-alone hook, Bebas-alone hook, Instrument Serif hook, thick outlined ExtraBold, Impact, Arial Black, Bahnschrift, Playfair for captions (except lyric reels), neon fills, more than one accent, text below y 1500 or in the right 130 px (FL rejected; MP s2; AD 46).
- No color grade on Jacob's footage; never add saturation, colorbalance warmth or teal-orange; no sheen or gradients on 307 red or El Coyote flat colors (MP s2; SK; memory).
- Never blur the text layer itself for a shadow (MP s2).
- Never interpolate steam, sparks or smoke; no rebound on people or smoke; whip pans only when the camera moved (MP s2; SK).
- No recursive zooms ("a bit too much"); no CapCut outro clip or template watermark; no third-party app logo in frame (MP s2; XP s4; LA s1).
- No cream-and-coral or neon green palettes; never the script word in Coyote tan (SK; FL).
- Never mix a meme beat and a build update in one reel; never two partners in one reel (AD 8; BS 5.5; PR 4c).
- Never a thank-you reel; never a "huge thanks to" opening; never a logo bumper (AD 31; BS 5.5; SE s3).

Sound
- No AI b-roll, no synthetic voice on a published reel, ever; Kokoro is a timer only (MP s4; AD 25; AU rule 1).

### 3.6 The 48 h read and the day-15 ranking (MM; OM s5)

| Metric | Floor | Goal | If it misses |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sends per reach | 1.0 | 2.0 | the format failed (trigger or recipient), change who it is for and line 1 |
| Skip rate | under 45 | under 35 | open on a person or a move, never a product or thank-you |
| Average watch / length | 50 percent | 60 percent | add the re-hook at 9 to 10 s or cut length |
| Likes per reach | 3 | 6 | low with high sends is fine |
| Saves | | | high saves, low sends = education; wrap in a tribe or argument frame |
| Comments | | | weakest signal; only matters on a gated reel (comments should exceed likes) |

Day-15 ranking: posts under 500 reach at 48 h do not rank; rank by sends per reach, ties within 0.2 to lower skip, then saves per reach; views and likes rank nothing; a post with comment rate over 1 percent and low sends is an argument reel, a separate lane. Winning cell = cell of the number one post; second cell = best cell in a different column within 50 percent of the winner; if the winner is a meme or skit cell and a build cell clears 1 percent, the build cell keeps the afternoon slot. Three straight posts under the day-15 median means the cell is spent. Change one variable per post.
