The objective changed. Until today the grade was sends per reach at 48 h with 30-day views as a side effect. From today every video is built to clear one million views. Nothing here replaces the craft rules (hook by 0.9 s, no on-screen ask, one lockup, no grade, song in-app, El Coyote only); it changes what the video is about, how long it is, and who it is for.

## 2. The two shapes that reach a million (and the one Instagram exception)

**Shape A, the story (60 to 120 s, narrated, his face late).** A problem a stranger understands in the first sentence, a number or a regret in the title, the answer withheld until the last ten seconds, one setting, cut every 2 to 4 s, the voice carries it. Oversteer, DriveHub, Dave's. For Rival this is the frame swap told as trouble: "I bought one of the last 1959 F600s left in Wyoming and it is trying to kill me", "A 7.3 everyone said could not make power", "What the frame swap has cost, to the dollar", the first start. **Superseded 2026-11-22 for the cut cadence only: change every four to eight seconds, cut only when the change needs one, one physical device per talking head (research/2026-11-22-REEL-EDITOR-DOCTRINE.md section 1 and 6). Everything else on this line stands.**

**Shape B, the skit (55 to 70 s, two people, one shot).** A universal work or truck situation played straight, the turn on the last line. joegotti and topherolive do nothing else and own the lane. For Jacob this is the parts counter and the shop floor with one coworker: the core charge, the backorder, "it just started doing that today", "did it fit". The one-man rule bends here: a coworker for sixty seconds at lunch, banked five at a time.

**The Instagram exception, the loop (6 to 12 s, no talk, universal).** Instagram counts replays, so a closed loop with a universal joke multiplies itself; his 506K is this. Keep it as the morning post, but the joke has to work for a stranger (money, pride, the wife, the boss), not only for the tribe.

What does not reach a million and is cut from the grid: thank-you and shout-out reels, partner announcements, build updates addressed to followers, slow montages with text cards, menu or card explainers about editing, anything whose first sentence needs a 7.3 to be understood. Named-tribe news stays as the share engine inside a Shape A story (the tribe named in line one, the stranger kept by the trouble), not as a 20 s reel on its own.

## 3. The "built for a million" checklist (every video, before it is cut)

1. **Stranger test.** Would a person who has never owned a diesel understand what is at stake by second three? If the answer needs a part name, rewrite the open.
2. **One universal emotion** named out loud: money lost, pride, regret, a thing that should not run but does, the boss, the wife, the old man who had one.
3. **A number or a price in the title line** ("$59", "67 years", "17-year-old", "one of the last", "to the dollar").
4. **The answer held to the end.** The last ten seconds carry the payoff; the first ten set the trouble. Re-hook at the third and the ninth of the way through.
5. **Length by shape:** story 60 to 120 s, skit 55 to 70 s, loop 6 to 12 s. Nothing 20 to 40 s; the data has zero millions there.
6. **A question that splits the comments** in the caption or the last line ("Who knows the story on this?", "Would you daily it?", "Is parts a trade?").
7. **Platform plan:** every video goes to TikTok and Shorts the same day as Instagram. The millions in the data are TikTok and YouTube; Instagram is where the loop multiplies. DriveHub's 14K median channel got 5.76M on Shorts.
8. **The craft rules still hold:** move on frame 1, first noun by 0.9 s, one lockup at most, no on-screen ask, no grade, song in-app, El Coyote only, real footage.
9. **The sends rule becomes the early warning, not the goal:** at 48 h a post on its way to a million shows sends per reach above 1 percent or average watch above 60 percent of length. Under both, it will not get there; learn and move on.
