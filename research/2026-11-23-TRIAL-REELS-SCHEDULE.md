# Trial Reels schedule, one a day (starts Tue 2026-11-24)

Jacob, 2026-11-23: "Come up with a trial reel schedule and strategy that works. I want to post every day at least once on trial reels." Rules and sources: `research/2026-11-23-TRIAL-REELS-PLAYBOOK.md`. This file is the calendar.

## The strategy in four lines

1. The trial is a THIRD post each day, on top of the AM and PM main-feed posts, so it has to cost almost no new shooting. Every trial is built from footage that already exists, or from one cab sit a week.
2. Four trial types rotate, picked from what this account's own numbers say works with strangers: the meme loop (the 10-mil meme did 506,827 views at 4.9 percent sends), the winner re-cut, the hook test, the recruiting reel.
3. A trial that beats the bar gets shared to everyone and takes the next PM main-feed slot. That is how trials make the main feed better instead of only adding work.
4. Read every trial at 24 hours, decide by a fixed rule, review the whole run on Mon Dec 7 (the same day as the day-15 ranking).

## The four types

| Type | What it is | Length | Shooting needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| MEME | A loop joke a stranger gets, same family as the 10-mil meme: one shot, one line of text, in-app song | 6 to 12 s | None; b-roll bank or an old clip |
| RECUT | A past winner with a new first two seconds and a new length | 6 to 15 s | None |
| HOOK TEST | Tomorrow's story post with a different opening line and first shot | same as the post | None; second open from the same footage |
| RECRUIT | Cab talking head: name the tribe, name the itch, say what the page is, "it's all on the page" | 15 to 25 s | One cab sit a week, 3 takes |

RECRUIT uses the spoken, trial-only invite (no text ask, no word "follow"). It stays in the schedule unless Jacob says no; if he does, those days become MEME.

## Daily clock (Mountain)

- 12:00 to 13:00, lunch: post the trial (Trial toggle ON, automatic share OFF, song in-app).
- Same sitting: open yesterday's trial, read the 24 h numbers, apply the rule below.
- AM 09:00 to 11:00 and PM 16:00 to 19:00 main-feed posts carry on per the 30-day matrix.

## The decision rule (24 hours after each trial)

- **Share to everyone** when a trial has at least 2 times the median views of the trials so far, or sends per reach at 2 percent or better. It takes the next PM slot and that day's planned PM idea moves back one day.
- **Leave it** otherwise. Never delete; it costs nothing sitting there.
- First five trials have no median yet: share only on 2 percent sends or 5,000 views.
- HOOK TEST: whichever open has the lower skip rate is the open the main-feed post uses.

## Jacob's time

- Daily: about 5 minutes at lunch (post one, read one).
- Weekly: one 15 minute cab sit on Sunday (three RECRUIT takes) and 10 minutes grabbing 5 to 7 second b-roll clips into Drive `VIDEO/broll`.
- Cutting: drop clips in Drive `VIDEO/raw`; each trial comes back in `VIDEO/finished/TRIALS week of <date>` named by the day it posts.

## What "works" means on Dec 7

- Follower growth in the 14 trial days beats the 14 days before (577 on 11-21 is the start line).
- At least two trials cleared the share-to-everyone bar.
- One type clearly leads on sends per reach; week 3 doubles it and drops the weakest.
If none of the three happen, trials drop to HOOK TEST only and the daily slot goes back to the main feed.

## Guard rails

- Never the same file twice, never more than two versions of one clip (duplicate trials are flagged as spam).
- Two trials a day at most, even on a good day.
- No trial for posts that need the followers (BD tag reel, giveaways, partner thank-yous).
- First step before any of this: confirm the Trial toggle shows on the share screen.
