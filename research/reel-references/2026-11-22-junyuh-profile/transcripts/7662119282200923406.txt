How can a video be posted twice, but one receives 6 million more views? This is how you use trial reels effectively. The only difference between these two videos are the hooks. Just changing a few words could be the difference of 6 million views. So for this video, I posted it three separate times testing a different hook in each. Once I landed it on the best hook, I actually left it in my trial reels tab to allow it to grow organically. Then I also reposted it on my main feed, so both can accrue millions of views separately. Therefore, trial reels are a great low pressure way of testing out these hooks to non-failures. From what I've seen across thousands of my students, changing just the hook is often enough to not be detected as a duplicate recently. And if a post ever does get flagged, just reorder a few clips or swap the audio and your gun.
