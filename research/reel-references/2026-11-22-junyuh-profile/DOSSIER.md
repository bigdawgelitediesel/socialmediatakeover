## 3. What he teaches (from the eleven transcripts)

1. **Call out one person. Nobody watches with a group of friends,** so "hey guys" and "hi everyone" are dead on arrival.
2. **"You" for what they want, "I" for the problems.** "You want to stay consistent without burning out. I used to say the same thing, but I couldn't go three days without quitting." That is how you sound like a friend, not a guru.
3. **Us versus the problem, never you versus them.** "You're not lazy, we just weren't taught how to be consistent." That is how followers become a community.
4. **Text tells the story, visuals amplify the emotion,** like a children's book. B-roll does not have to be from the exact moment; film a new shot that carries the same feeling.
5. **Watch your own video like a creator, not a consumer:** where did you feel called out, where did you get curious, what kept you watching.
6. **Become the niche, do not choose one.** Choosing a topic grows fast for a month and then traps you; being the person lets the audience grow with you. "They followed the topic, not me" is the failure case.
7. **Post every day, create a few days a week:** batch, repurpose, mix in low-effort posts.
8. **Only brands that fit the image; build your own products** because you know what the audience wants.
9. **Trial Reels for hooks:** the same video posted three times with three hooks, 6M views between the best and worst; leave the winner in the Trials tab to grow and repost it to the main feed; changing the hook is enough to avoid the duplicate flag, and if flagged, reorder two clips or swap the audio.
10. **Intonation:** it is not what you say, it is how you say it; the rise and fall decides which word the viewer hears.
11. **Reduce the noise to sharpen the ideas:** the most visible people are the hardest to reach.
12. **Talk to yourself in the third person** to see your own life as a story; you think your life is boring because you are too close to it.
13. **The sentimental synthesizer:** meaning lives in the mundane objects (the coffee cup you shared with your dad); the stories are already there.
14. **Visuals: eye line punched in, caption under the eyes, hook tied to the eye line, a stroke on text only over a busy background.**
