## Why it worked

1. **The first sentence is a question the audience has already asked.** Everyone in his lane has fed an AI a hook video and got slop back. Recognition, then the diagnosis in one line: steps are not thinking.
2. **The card is the cut, again.** The steps-versus-why card and the mind map carry the idea; the face carries the person. Same mechanism as Mariah's notebook, Sam Gaudet's thumbnails and Kier's chapter titles, drawn instead of written.
3. **The receipt is on screen.** The Insights panel with the dates, 1.8M and 15.3K, held for four seconds while he says the numbers. Caleb's credibility rule in one insert.
4. **The gate.** 31K comments on 11.6K likes. The reach came from the keyword funnel, which the house caps at one post in four with a real free deliverable. Note that the deliverable here is real and free, which is why it is not a list grab in the way Tom's was.

## The one idea that matters for El Coyote

"It just blindly copies the steps but completely misses the thinking behind them." That sentence is the brief for the streamline editor Jacob asked for. The library has 114 numbered rules and a doctrine; applied as steps they would produce the same reel every day, which is the AI tell. Applied as thinking they produce a different reel from the same seat. So:

- `skill/el-coyote/PRINCIPLES.md` (2026-11-23) states each principle with the thinking behind it and the way it looks when copied as a step, so the editor and any agent can audit a script against the why, not the shape.
- `tools/streamline.py` plans a cut from word timings, then challenges its own plan: the doctrine counter-arguments for each decision, a sameness check against the last three episodes on six axes (opener type, set, device, lockup style, tone, length) with a rotation suggestion, and a thinking test of eight questions that must be answered before render. Then it renders and lints.
- Remy's three named principles map onto ours: curiosity loop (one loop opened in sentence one, closed last), specificity (Humm's five, the concrete noun by 0.9 s), line one sells line two (but and therefore, never and then).

## What does not transfer

- The keyword gate at that ratio. One in four posts, caption only, a real deliverable.
- Content about content. Jacob's version of "teach it how to think" is a parts-counter principle: the fix is upstream of the part that keeps dying.
