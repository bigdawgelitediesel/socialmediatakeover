# @justyn.ai editing style, catalogued from 11 videos (2026-11-20)

Jacob: "I want you editing like this. Check this whole profile out." Videos and 2 fps sheets are in this folder; the single IG reel is in `../2026-11-20-justyn-ai-claude-edited-this/`.

## The system in one paragraph
A talking head shot in two or three framings, cut on sentence breaks, and a layer of branded motion graphics that illustrates every noun the moment it is spoken. One palette (deep forest green, cream, burgundy, a mustard accent), one serif display family plus a plain sans for UI mock cards, captions as short cream-on-green pills with the key word in italic or a lighter tint. Something new happens every 1.5 to 3 seconds. The graphics are UI mockups and print objects (prompt bars, comment boxes, timelines, contact sheets, magazine covers, banknotes, screenplay pages), never stock "effects". He names them himself in a sidebar list on the "20 effects in 20 seconds" videos.

## Effect catalog (his names, what they are, how often he uses them)
Hook and title
- Kinetic hook: 2 to 4 words slam in one at a time, serif caps, slight overlap, 1 to 1.5 s.
- Gravity drop: a word falls in from above and settles with a small bounce; the italic qualifier ("gravity") floats above it.
- Giant captions: one huge word fills the width, next phrase tucks under it.
- Title card: full-frame brand-green card, serif title, tiny timestamp label top-left, 0.5 s.
- Knockout captions: cream card with the word cut out (knocked out) of a dark block.
- Letter dive / Picture dive: a giant letter or the video itself scales from 400 percent down into place.
- Magazine cover: the frame becomes a magazine cover with masthead, barcode, cover lines.
- Serif stamp / Swirl stamp: a word stamps on with a rubber-stamp impact.
- Word stamp: ZERO, HUGE, BETTER RESULT, built letter by letter, sometimes mirrored echo behind.
- Chapter numerals: 1 2 3 stack in as huge serif numbers with a micro label ("PICK THE RIGHT").

Cards that illustrate nouns (the heart of it)
- Prompt bar: a chat input that types the words he says ("make it moody"), then shows "sent".
- Comment drop / Comment CTA: a comment box slides in, "EDIT" types itself, a reply appears; the comments stack for testimonials.
- Playing grid / Reel feed / Reel cascade: thumbnails of other reels fan or cascade in when he says "this video, this video".
- Video card: a small framed clip ("the raw take") with a label.
- Storyboard deck / Contact sheet hook / Grease pencil sheet: a grid of frames, a contact sheet with grease-pencil circles and "dead air" crossed out.
- NLE timeline: a fake editor timeline with clips and a playhead.
- File drop: a dashed drop zone, a file lands, a filename chip appears.
- Icon strike: app icons (CapCut, Edits, InShot, Premiere) appear and get struck through one by one.
- Keycaps: keyboard keys tumble in (Q, space, cmd).
- Predict keys: a phone keyboard with predictive words.
- Grade slider / Before-After: a vertical wipe handle sweeps the color grade across his face.
- Eyedropper grade: a row of hex swatches sampled from the frame.
- Day dial / Effort dial / Lens ring: a dial graphic turns to a value (MAX).
- Calendar fill: a week grid fills with green blocks for "free up your time".
- Requirements strike list: a card with lines that get crossed out.
- Waitlist card: a form where "you" writes itself on line No. 1.
- Name tag: HELLO my name is, handwritten.
- Model dropdown: a UI select opens and picks the item.
- Progress bar: "RENDERING VIDEO 21 percent" to 100.
- Banknote: his face engraved on a bill with the number counting up.
- Voice note: a waveform pill with play button.
- Post wall / Head stack / Clone row: tiled thumbnails, stacked heads, duplicated frames.
- Screenplay page: a cream page in Courier, "INT. GREEN ROOM - DAY", his lines.
- Live markup / Sketch words / Grease pencil: pink hand-drawn circles and words over the frame.
- Code body: his torso filled with scrolling code (texture fill).
- Animated chart: a line chart draws itself with labels (economy video), serif italic "boom".

Motion and cuts
- Jiggle delete: a clip wiggles like an iOS icon and is deleted.
- Blade slice, Paper tear, Blow away, Layer explode (the frame splits into stacked glass layers), Grid collapse, Impact frame, Dolly zoom, Rack focus, Live cursor (a mouse cursor draws a selection on him), Press front.
- Between shots: hard cuts on sentence ends, occasional 4 to 6 frame directional blur, picture dive into the next room.
- Black and white beat: desaturate for the "scary part", color returns on the payoff.

Captions
- Serif, sentence case, one short phrase (3 to 5 words), cream on a dark green rounded pill, bottom third, the key word italic or lighter. A second sans style (white bold with dark outline pill) for the plainer videos. Never a whole sentence, never word-by-word color flashes.

Audio
- His voice, clean, plus soft UI sounds on the cards (type clicks, a pop on arrivals) and a low music bed. SFX exist but are tied to objects, not to cuts.

## Translation to El Coyote
Palette: black #0b0b0c base, cream #efe9dc type, Coyote tan #c9b38c accent, oxblood #7a1f1f for cards and stamps, yellow #F7D43C only in the Combo A script word. Type: Anton for block titles, Kaushan Script for the one brush word, Poppins (Light/Medium/Italic) for UI cards and labels, Instrument Serif Italic for the single "classy" word. Cards for Rival: part photos, parts.json rows, partner logos, the portal, Fullbay invoice lines, torque specs, the SEMA countdown, a Marketplace listing, a shop floor plan, a calendar of build days. Caption pill: Poppins Medium cream on oxblood, key word in tan italic. The "sidebar list" trick is also usable: a running list of build items that ticks off as he talks.

## Process he states on camera (adopt)
1. Strongest model, effort max. 2. Brand style guide plus sample reels you like, so the look is fixed before editing. 3. A shot-by-shot artboard for approval before rendering. 4. Notes are given with timestamps and the exact words spoken.
