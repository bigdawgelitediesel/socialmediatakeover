## 4. The comment-for-DM growth engine

What the viewer sees: every resource reel ends with the spoken line 'Comment "KEYWORD" and I'll DM you ...' while the screen shows a fake Instagram comment box with the keyword typed in and hearted, then a fake DM notification card that previews exactly what arrives ("Messages · now, Your 9 free repos" with nine numbered chips). What runs it:

- The automation tool "was never named in the chat or the repo. The owner only asks for the DM text 'so that i can put in automation'." (`hooks-ctas-automations.md` 3.1). So ManyChat or equivalent, unconfirmed.
- They also built their own: PR #11 (2026-10-25, unmerged) adds `automation/instagram-dm/`, a TypeScript service on the official Meta Graph API with OAuth, webhook signature verification, keyword rules, comment-to-DM, auto-replies and welcome flows, plus a YAML rules file. And they sell "DM automation" to clients (service page /social-media-automation). Source: https://github.com/nxtlvlsocial1-web/Nxtlvl-Social/pull/11.
- DM content rule (verbatim from their CLAUDE.md): "The keyword auto-DM (automation) is only the resource link and its setup steps, nothing else: no greeting, buttons, comment replies, follow line or caveats." Shape: first line "<Name>: <LINK>" then three numbered steps. Example DM for the SCRIPT keyword: "Creedom: [LINK] 1. Open the link and connect your Instagram account. 2. Type @write-script in the chat box. 3. Tell it what your reel is about and send."
- Caption rule (verbatim): "Start every post description with the video's CTA keyword line ... then the hook and the body; don't repeat the CTA further down." End with "up to 5 hashtags and then one plain line of relevant SEO keywords (the phrases people search for the topic, comma separated)." Client captions have a no-emoji rule; the REPO caption on @ai.nxtlvl uses emojis freely, so the lab account does not follow the client rule.
- Reach posts vs lead posts (their own 12-creator audit, 2026-11-12): "Keep reach posts and lead posts separate. Use the keyword only on resource posts: repos, prompt packs, guides. Wonder and news posts should end on a question or 'follow', so the keyword doesn't cost them reach." Also: "Saves come from lists, codes and prompts written out in the caption ... Shares come from wonder clips and news that affects the viewer. Comments come from the keyword CTA and from direct questions." Length by format: "Lists and codes: 19-26 s. Repo explainers: 23-41 s. Tutorials: 60-90 s."
- Lead magnets are 4:5 PDFs (1080x1350, 6 to 10 pages: cover, rules page, card pages, checklist) built from JSON with Playwright. As of 2026-11-17 not one PDF or partner link was actually hosted ("the Creedom link was asked for at least ten times ... and never sent"), which means several keyword reels went out with no DM behind them. Source: `open-items.md` section 2.
- On YouTube the mechanic cannot work (no comment-triggered DMs), so the CTA mutates to 'Comment "CLIPS" and grab the free workflow + DM template from the link on my channel page (top link)'. The description still has the placeholder "[your Google Drive link]" in it.

Hook rules they run every script through (verbatim, `hooks-ctas-automations.md` 1.1-1.2): "Start at 70, not 0 (0-5 s). The hook drops the viewer into the middle of a story that has already started." "First peak at 90 (by 5-15 s). A contrast moment, a contrarian statement or a shocking stat." "Release, then peak again ... No single climax in the middle." "Close on the CTA ... straight after the last peak." Owner: "try something miraculous in the first 6-7 seconds that really feels like 'whoa what is happening in this reel'." Five hook formulas from their competitor audit: news plus a fix within a day; GitHub stars as proof; secret codes or a numbered list; the best tool for each job (the most-saved format in their sample); disbelief ("This shouldn't be possible").

## 7. Editing style, from frames actually viewed

### 7a. The REPO reel (sheet-01..03 in the sibling folder, 52 frames at 1 fps)

- **Canvas:** warm cream paper, roughly #EDE8E0, soft vignette, no texture, no gradient drift. It never changes for 52 s.
- **Palette:** one coral accent (about #E8654A) for the mascot, icon tiles, progress dots, highlight pills and the single iris wipe; green (#2E9E5B range) only for FREE stamps, OPEN SOURCE tags and the "ALL FREE ON GITHUB" ribbon; red only for PAID tags; near-black ink for text and card borders. Four colors total.
- **Cards:** white, 2 px dark border, hard offset drop shadow (down-right, no blur), 20 px radius. Neo-brutalist-lite. Inside: coral square icon tile at top-left, repo name in a heavy grotesk (Inter 700/800 class), `owner/repo` in a small monospace under it, a "★ 32.2K stars" counter pill in mono, an OPEN SOURCE green outline tag, then feature chips (pale coral fill, dark text, pill shape) that pop in on the spoken word, a mono footnote line, and a rotated green FREE sticker slapped on the bottom-right corner.
- **Caption band:** one short phrase at y about 220, grey mid-weight sans, revealed word by word on the voice. Exactly one accent word per phrase in Instrument Serif Italic on a coral highlight pill ("Open Generative AI.", "cinematic", "fully local,", "own server.", "one click.", "REPO", "links."). The accent is always the payoff noun. There are no bottom captions at all (their house rule: "remove the bottom captions ... keep the above caption type texts").
- **HUD:** top-left "0/9" counter pill plus nine dots that fill coral one per item; top-right "@ai.nxtlvl" in small mono. Every frame carries the handle.
- **Mascot:** a 60 px coral pixel robot with a thought bubble that parks on the top-right corner of the current card. It is the only "character" and it never speaks.
- **Hook frame (0 to 2 s):** a 3x3 grid of real app logos (Higgsfield, ElevenLabs, Semrush, Notion, Wispr Flow, Asana, Calendly, Intercom, Loom) in white tiles, each with a red PAID tag. Frame 0 already shows the full grid and the first caption words. By 3 s every logo is struck through and a green FREE tag stamps on, left to right, top to bottom.
- **Motion grammar:** zero hard cuts in 52 s (PySceneDetect found one scene). Instead 8 to 11 element changes per 10 s: card drops in from below with a 12-frame overshoot, chips scale from 0.8 over 6 frames, strike-throughs wipe left to right over 8 frames, star counters tween over about 18 frames, the OpenSEO card enters "ghosted" at 30 percent opacity for 0.3 s then resolves. One full-frame transition only: a coral circle iris at 47.0 to 47.6 s before the CTA. Then a 2.6 s silent hold on the final list.
- **Framing:** everything in a centered column 760 to 940 px wide, content between y 320 and 1250, clear of the IG UI. Top 250 and bottom 400 px are empty, which matches their written safe-zone rule.
- **Sound:** synthetic female voice, 152 words per minute, mastered to -14.3 LUFS; no detectable music bed or SFX hits in this one (their later reels add SFX on "the counter, strikes, row ticks").
- **Type stack observed:** heavy grotesk for names, monospace for metadata and the HUD, serif italic for the accent word. This matches the stack in their style notes: "Inter 700/800 titles with tight tracking (-0.035 to -0.045 em), Instrument Serif Italic accent words, JetBrains Mono Bold labels."
