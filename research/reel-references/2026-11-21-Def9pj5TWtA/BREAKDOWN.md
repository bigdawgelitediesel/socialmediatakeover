## The format (the thing to learn first)

A menu reel. He sits in a dark room, holds his hand up, and a phone-shaped card floats where his hand is. Inside the card, one named effect plays for about one second while he says its name. Seventeen names in 16.5 s, then a 10 s pitch.

Fixed frame furniture, on from frame 1 to 16.5 s:
- Title, top center, two lines: "17 video effects / claude can create", bold geometric sans about 54 px, white, the number in yellow (#F5E642 range).
- Sidebar, two columns under the title, left column 01 to 09, right column 10 to 17: tiny numbered list (about 22 px, grey 60 percent) of the effect names. The current item turns yellow and bold the moment he says it. This is the HUD: progress, menu and promise in one object, and it tells the viewer "there are 17, stay".
- The phone card: 9:16 rounded card about 330 px wide at x 375 to 705, y 690 to 1370, white 6 px border, soft shadow, slight float (1 to 2 px bob). Top-left badge "01/17" (white number, grey total) in a dark pill. The card never cuts; its content swaps with the effect.
- Him: talking-head plate darkened about 25 percent with a soft vignette so the card and title pop. He gestures at the card.

Timing: shots of 0.67 to 1.27 s (median 0.93 s). Voice 152 to 160 wpm. The effect names are the entire script for 16.5 s, which is why it holds: no sentence to process, just a list and a picture per item.

Second half (16.5 to 27.2 s): the furniture disappears, full-frame talking head, plain white bold captions, 2 to 5 words, yellow accent word, "26" slams in as a huge yellow numeral, "video effects" in yellow, then a mock comment thread card and a mock profile card slide up from the bottom (his profile with posts grid) as he says "comment studio and I'll send you the waitlist, make sure to follow". Ends on the profile card, no fade.

## What this reel teaches beyond the effects

1. **The menu reel.** A sidebar list plus a counter plus one demo per second is a complete format. It works because the list is the script, the counter is the promise, and each item is a tiny payoff. 34k comments came from "number 14 is my favorite, what's yours" (a pick-one prompt) stacked on the STUDIO gate.
2. **The phone card as a stage.** Showing the effect inside a small card next to the presenter keeps him on screen the whole time (face retention) while the card carries the novelty. It also hides the seams: a 330 px card forgives rough compositing.
3. **The matte unlocks half the vocabulary.** We already run RVM (tools/matte.py). Frame break, couch wall, chart grow, green screen, paper cut, doodle boil, vertigo and cover story are all "something behind him or an edge around him".
4. **Yellow is his one accent, and it is used for exactly three jobs**: current item, accent word, hero number. That restraint is what reads as designed.
5. **Second-half discipline.** Furniture off, plain captions, one numeral, mock comment and profile cards as the CTA visuals. Note for us: Jacob's rule is no on-screen CTA, so for El Coyote the menu half is the usable part and the pitch half is not.
