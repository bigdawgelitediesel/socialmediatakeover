# Caption and title audit of the reference reels (2026-11-21)

What the winners actually burn on screen, measured frame by frame, against the El Coyote typography spec in `research/2026-11-20-EDITING-MASTER-PLAYBOOK.md` (Typography section), the lockups in `research/FONT-LIBRARY.md`, and the `PlainCaption` / `CaptionPill` components in `research/tools-snapshot/rival-reels/src/`.

55 reels read (14 diesel winners, 20 card explainers, 20 talking-head AI creators, plus our own Harley Trade cut as the baseline). 220 crops at native 1920-frame scale sit in `crops/` (`<slug>-1..4.png` captions, `<slug>-hook.png` titles, `<slug>-label.png` card furniture).

## 1. Method and what the numbers mean

- Every frame was normalised to 1080x1920 before measuring (ffmpeg `scale=1080:1920:flags=lanczos,crop=1080:600:0:Y`), so every px below is a px in our delivery frame. Sources at 576x1024 and 720x1280 (most TikTok mirrors) were upscaled, which adds 1 to 2 px of blur to a band edge; 1440x2560 (ultraskills) was downscaled. Landscape sources (09 tomsrefurb, 14 foose) are reported as a fraction of frame height.
- A row-profile script (`measure.py`, scratch) found the text bands (rows of bright low-saturation pixels, or dark pixels for text on light plates), reported band height, x extent, centre y, colour buckets and a dark-ring ratio around the glyphs. Every crop was then read by eye to classify font, weight, case, accent and stroke, and to throw out bands inflated by a white shirt or a ceiling light (noted per row).
- Cap height: all-caps text = band height; mixed case with descenders = 0.75 x band; mixed without descenders = 0.95 x band; checked by eye on the crop. "est." marks an eye judgement.
- Centre y = centre of the caption block in the 1920 frame. "Over face" = the text crosses the face box, not merely sits in the same shot.
- Calibration of our own fonts (rendered with ffmpeg drawtext, measured the same way), so spec font sizes can be compared with measured cap heights:

| Font and size in our spec or kit | Cap height on 1920 |
|---|---|
| Poppins SemiBold 60 (PlainCaption default) | 42 px (x-height 33) |
| Poppins SemiBold 68 / 72 / 80 | 47 / 51 / 56 px |
| Poppins Medium 62 / 28 | 43 / 20 px |
| Montserrat SemiBold 66 (old Cap style) | 47 px |
| Montserrat ExtraBold 96 (HookAlt) | 67 px |
| Oswald Bold 112 (old Hook style) | 91 px |
| Archivo Black 260 (MEGANTE) / 110 | 179 / 76 px |
| Anton 380 (LOUDER block word) | 326 px |
| Instrument Serif Italic 70 | 51 px |

Rule of thumb: Poppins cap = 0.70 x font size, Oswald = 0.81, Anton = 0.86, Instrument Serif = 0.73.

Limits: 55 reels from one week of pulls, chosen for performance and for being the accounts Jacob pointed at, not a random sample. tf-five-clips and part of us-hey-siri were measured from the band profile with a partial eyeball. The dark-ring stat cannot tell a shadow from a dark background behind the text, so stroke calls are visual.

## 5. Proposed v2 caption spec (three El Coyote cases)

All values in 1080x1920 px. Fonts are the static files already in `research/fonts/` and `VIDEO/tools/fonts/`. One accent colour per reel (tan #C9B38C; yellow #F7D43C only inside the LOUDER lockup). No stroke on any text, ever. No per-word colour change. No pill under a talking-head caption. No on-screen call to action, no end card.

### Case 1. Talking head over footage (Jacob to camera, shop or truck)

Caption (`PlainCaption`, `captionStyle: "plain"`):
- Font: Poppins SemiBold, 68 px (cap 47). Range 64 to 72. When the framing is wide and the face is small (head under 300 px tall) go to 80 px (cap 56, the tenfoldmarc size). Never under 64.
- Case: sentence case, as spoken. No caps lines.
- Group: 2 to 4 words, 24 characters max, one line; a second line only when a 5 or 6 word phrase will not split on meaning. Readability floor 0.3 s per word plus 0.6 s. Zero gaps between groups.
- Colour: white #FFFFFF, tracking -0.5 px (Poppins is already tight).
- Shadow (one CSS layer, two shadows): `0 3px 5px rgba(0,0,0,0.65), 0 10px 28px rgba(0,0,0,0.40)`. The first is the chandler offset, the second is our soft spread. No WebkitTextStroke, no outline.
- Position: block centre y 1190 by default (paddingTop 1150 at 68 px with lineHeight 1.2). Rule from the face tracker: block top at least 120 px below the chin; allowed centre window 1050 to 1360; block bottom never below 1400. Tight shot with the chin below 1150: drop to centre 1330 (the justyn band). Face in the upper left (PiP-style framing): centre 1238 is fine. Never on the face.
- Entry: whole group rises 10 px with a 120 ms blur-in (the word-rise-with-blur already in the kit), ease-out; exit 80 ms fade or hard swap when the next group starts. No per-word motion.
- Accent: none by default. When one word earns it (the number, the part name, the punchline noun), that word turns tan, same size, same line, one per phrase, at most three per reel. Second option, one beat per reel: the tenfoldmarc pop, the payoff word drops in 90 px below the white line at 1.7x (Poppins SemiBold 116, cap 81) in tan with a 120 ms blur-in while the white line stays. The coral filled box (`hiColor`) stays in the kit for comedic beats only; it is no longer the default accent.
- Contrast fallback over bright footage: 45 pct black gradient from y 1000 to 1600 (unchanged), applied only when the mean luma of the caption zone is above 0.55. Never a stroke, never a pill.

Hook title on a talking head (used when the reel has no lockup):
- Two-line question or hater quote, Poppins SemiBold 72 px (cap 51), white, sentence case, centred at x 540, block centre y 320, above the head (needs the head top below about 450; otherwise use a lockup instead, or place the title in the empty side of the frame). One word tan.
- Hater-quote variant (the Stradman structure, a title, so a pill is allowed): same text in a black pill `rgba(0,0,0,0.9)`, radius 18, padding 16 px / 28 px, line pitch 1.3, block centre y 480, static through the setup, removed on the reveal. Max 11 words over 3 lines.
- Both: 120 ms fade plus 140 ms scale from 0.94. On screen 2.5 to 4 s. Never together with a lockup.
- The locked lockups (LOUDER, MEGANTE, TROPI) stay as specified in FONT-LIBRARY for memes and Rival episodes.

Labels and partner tags (`tooltag`, badges): Poppins Medium 28 px (cap 20) caps, tracking +8, tan, in a 1.5 px tan-bordered pill (padding 8/14, radius 8) top-left at x 72, y 300 for episode or series badges; or icon plus name at top centre (chandler) with the name in Poppins SemiBold 60. Nothing in the right 130 px. Text the viewer must read: 40 px floor.

### Case 2. Card reel on black or paper (`CardReel`, menu reels, parts lists)

Plate: black #0b0b0c with the kit grain, or paper #EFE9DC with a 2 px #D9D2C3 dot grid at 48 px pitch (the nxtlvl plate). No pure white plate.

Voice caption (the phrase that tracks the voice):
- Font: Poppins Medium 62 px (cap 43) on paper in ink #141414; Poppins SemiBold 62 px white on black. Sentence case.
- Group: 2 to 4 words, one line, whole phrase appears per group (per-group on paper, the nxtlvl way). On black plates the speedy per-word build is allowed: words appear as spoken, earlier words go to #9A9A9A, never a bright colour on the current word.
- Position: block centre y 360 (window 290 to 420) when the card or object sits below it, which is the default layout; centre y 1440 only when a tall phone or screen mock owns the middle of the frame. Never both positions in one reel.
- Shadow: none on plates.
- Accent (one per phrase): the payoff phrase in Instrument Serif Italic 62 px (cap 45) inside a selection box: fill tan at 22 pct alpha, 1.5 px tan border, two 10 px square handles at mid-left and mid-right, padding 6 px / 16 px, radius 4. On black the same with white text and tan border. This is the one place the serif lives.

Hook title:
- Black plate: Archivo Black 110 px (cap 76) caps, 2 lines, white, one word tan, block centre y 400, no glow, no gradient. 
- Paper plate: no bigger type; the same 62 px sans as the body plus the HUD (progress squares 48 px at y 290, "1/9" counter) carries the hook. The question line sits at y 405.
- Fixed furniture: badge top-left at y 290 in Poppins Medium 26 px caps tracked +8 inside a 1.5 px ink-bordered pill; handle "@elcoyotediesel" top-right in 24 px; card titles Archivo Black 72 px with sub lines at 26 px; chips 24 px; sidebar list rows 30 px at 64 px pitch, current item ink on a tan bar, others at 40 pct grey; indices 22 px mono. - Timings unchanged from the addendum (card drop 0.35 s back.out, chips 0.2 to 0.3 s, iris 0.6 s).

### Case 3. Meme reel (fast tier, Jacob's footage, real audio, 8 to 22 s)

- Burned text: one lockup on the hook (Combo A LOUDER default, B or C by mood), 2.5 to 3 s, in the empty half of the frame with Jacob low and whole. Nothing else burned in. The diesel data backs this: 10 of 14 winners have no text at all and the four that do have exactly one text object.

- Punchline text, only when the joke is spoken and the audio alone will not carry it: one case 1 caption group (Poppins SemiBold 68, white, shadow, centre 1190 or chin plus 120), held 0.6 to 1.2 s after the line. No running captions through the body.
- No caption band, no series tag, no location pill, no per-word anything. (The Harley cut's 20 px grey tag was the least legible text in all 55 reels; the series lives in the MEGANTE sub line on episodes and in caption line 1 on memes.)
