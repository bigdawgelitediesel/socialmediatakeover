## The three fonts (he names each one on screen, set in itself)

| # | Font | What he says | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Impact** | "It's bold, it's bulky, and my favorite part about it, it's a little compressed. So naturally it fits very well whenever I'm editing on 9x16." | The Windows and Mac system font. |
| 2 | **Snell Roundhand** | "The beauty of this font is unmatched." | A formal round-hand script. Linotype, paid; bundled with macOS and iOS. |
| 3 | **Rocktown** | "It literally looks like someone painted it... I use this across 90% of my edits... adding some personality to your text." | A dry-brush caps font by Khurasan. Free for personal use; commercial use needs the paid licence. |

His caption says all three came free from DaFont. That does not hold for an account with partners: Snell Roundhand is a paid
font and Rocktown is personal-use only. Stand-ins used here: Pinyon Script (SIL OFL) and Permanent Marker (Apache 2.0), compared
side by side with crops of his frames before choosing.

## How he uses them

- **Pairing is the whole point:** "If you're not pairing your fonts, then what are you doing?" The hook title is two stacked Impact
  lines (FONTS yellow, FOR YOUR red) with a white brush word (VIDEOS?) laid across them at an angle. Later: GAME in red Impact with OVER in white brush across it.
  His own cover examples at 41 s do the same: MY TOP 3 / ESSENTIALS / FOR CONTENT CREATION.
- **Colours:** yellow about `#FDC43A`, red about `#F04625`, white. Measured off his frames.
- **Placement:** every title sits in the top third, above his head. Nothing touches his face.
- **Captions:** small white bold serif, a few words at a time, at chest height. Not one of the three fonts.
- **The reel itself:** two characters played by the same man (the creator and "my editor"), swap at 6 s, one seated shot,
  one cut-out circle transition at 30 s, three of his own covers on a red card at 41 s. Ends on his logo card.
