A `compare` overlay that puts them in two tilted cards over a dark field is a reusable "how this reel was made" post, and the one honest way to show the editing work without a face-to-camera pitch. 2. **The hater pill as the hook** stacks with the title: the objection on top, the proof below, no voice needed. Matches the hater-quote rule from the diesel lanes (Stradman) and the caption-audit rule that a title pill is allowed for a quote, never for a caption.
3. **Highlighter scribble as the accent** (a hand-drawn lime stroke under the payoff noun and a scribble circle around the face) is the same job as our tan accent word, drawn instead of typed. Doable in Remotion with an SVG path and stroke-dashoffset. Low priority; the tan word is the house accent.
4. **Three type voices in one lockup** (small sans, condensed block, serif italic) is our LOUDER grammar; nothing to change.
5. **The permission-prompt terminal card** is an in-joke for the AI-tools crowd. Not for El Coyote.
