## Why it worked (6K likes, 248 comments, 88K account)

1. **The sample is the hook.** "112 reels that hit 1M views" is a number and a method in the first line. The self-deprecation ("I must not have a life") lowers status before the credential ("thousands of videos go through me"), the order the trust research recommends.
2. **A fair test, stated.** Excluding famous people, memes and nudity is the line that makes the rest believable.
3. **Numbered chapters are the whole edit.** One take, ten title changes. He demonstrates finding 1 (talking immediately) and finding 6 (readable text never under the UI) with his own frame.
4. **The identity hook example is concrete.** "What type of person is most likely to become a CEO" is the one line people quote in the comments.
5. **The comment offer in the caption is a real deliverable** (a diagnosis of your video), and 248 comments is what that buys at 88K.
