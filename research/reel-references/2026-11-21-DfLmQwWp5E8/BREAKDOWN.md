## What is new for the kit (ported 2026-11-21, components/Paper.tsx, test in tools-snapshot/rival-reels/paper-test.props.json)
1. **WindowStack** `winstack`: browser windows fan in one per word, offset down-right, dark label pill in the corner. Shipped defaults w 600, dx 120, dy 170 so three windows end at x 900 inside the right safe line.
2. **PinThumb** `pinthumb`: the real photo lands as a polaroid with a script note ("one real picture"), then shrinks to a corner thumbnail with a "✓ reference" tag that tracks the image height and stays. For Rival the real photo of the part stays pinned while the explanation runs.
3. **Terminal** `terminal`: dark window, command types at 2 frames per character, optional highlight span, green "✓ result" line 8 frames after the last letter.
4. **RepoCard** `repo`: owner / name / description / ★ / licence, rotated FREE stamp (power4.out from scale 2.2), "built by @handle" credit pill. The credit pill is the repostability move: name the builder on screen and he reshares (addendum rule on partner repostability).
5. **SpecChips** `specchips`: white chips, bold number + serif italic unit, back.out(3) pops on their words. The "from real numbers" proof beat.
6. **NotePill** `notepill`: one small dark annotation with a highlighted word ("every gauge = live").
7. **PlainCaption** gained `accentFont` ("serif" | "script") so the accent word switches face as well as colour, and `ink` for dark text on paper; both are wired through the caption schema in RivalReel and CardReel (`variant`, `accentFont`, `ink` per caption line).
Not ported: the pixel mascot (a brand character; El Coyote has no sticker character yet, and the coyote mark would have to be redrawn as one), the generated confetti on the gate, the WITHOUT/WITH toy illustration (CompareCards already covers the two-card compare with real footage).

## What to take for El Coyote
- **"Someone just built..." is third-party news framing**, the same engine as the named-tribe news rule (DIESEL-HOUR 50 to 53): the reel is about someone else's work, credited on screen and in the caption, which is why the builder and his followers carry it. Diesel version: "Someone just put 600 hp through a stock 7.3 bottom end" with the builder's handle on a RepoCard-style credit card.
- **Paper explainer = the parts-counter format without a camera**: CardReel on "paper", Fullbay or portal screen captures in windows, the real photo pinned, spec chips under the part. Zero setup, one voice take. This is the Torsten Roscoe lesson (DeuZvcPqAGV) with better furniture.
- **Pin the real picture.** Any reel that explains a part shows the actual part photo, then keeps it in the corner. It also answers the no-AI-imagery rule on screen: the reference is visibly real.
- **Spec chips carry the credential.** Numbers with units, popping on the spoken word, under the thing they describe. Jacob's numbers: 238 cc, 38R, 0.030 over, 600 hp, 7,400 rpm, SEMA November 2 to 5, 2027.
- **Teardown itself is a Rival asset candidate, Jacob's call.** A 7.3 Powerstroke sim built from the real build sheet (compound turbo plumbing, the regulated return, the HPOP) with the real engine photo as the reference would give the build series a cut-open explainer no other 7.3 account has.
