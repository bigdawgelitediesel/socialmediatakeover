## The format (his four steps, from the on-screen doc at 0:37)

1. Call out the target audience in the first second, by name, so the right person stops and the wrong one scrolls.
2. Name their main pain point: one specific frustration.
3. Tease the result you deliver: the benefit and a hint that your page is where they get it.
4. Invite them in: a plain spoken ask to check the profile and follow. Match the energy of the reel, never robotic.
Length 15 to 30 s. Posted as a Trial Reel, so only non-followers see it and it never lands on the grid.

His own example (0:17 to 0:32): "If you're a content creator who wants to be told exactly how to blow up not just your view metrics but also how to make money with not that many views, without stupid theory, just tactical real world strategies you can implement instantly, take a look at my videos and make sure to follow."

The condition he attaches: "If your profile is optimized and your content is actually good, you're going to grow like never before." The trial is a billboard; the profile closes.

## What to take for El Coyote

- The format is a 15 to 30 s recruiting reel aimed at strangers only. On this account that means: name the tribe in the first second ("If you're a 7.3 guy", "If you've ever pulled a cab off a frame"), name the itch (nobody shows the real cost, the real failures), tease what the page is (a 1959 F600 going onto a Super Duty frame with a 7.3, headed for SEMA 2027, every dollar shown), then the invite.
- CONFLICT with house rules: Jacob's rule is no follow ask, ever, and no on-screen CTA. A trial reel never reaches followers or the grid, which is the argument for a trial-only exception with a SPOKEN invite to look at the page (no burned-in CTA, no "follow"). That exception is Jacob's call; until he says yes, the trial version ends on "it's all on the page" and stops.
- The condition is the real lesson: the profile has to convert. Finish the profile spec (name field, bio line, three pins, highlights) before running any of these.
- 20 to 40 s is the length the million-view rule bans for main-feed posts. Trials are a different job (profile visits from strangers), so 15 to 25 s is fine there; log it as its own cell.
