Yes. Pick any edit you like and Claude recreates it on your own A-roll: your footage or your client's. We have a whole module on exactly this.

Won't AI edits look generic?

They do when Claude guesses. That's what EDITA BRAIN fixes: Claude measures a real reference frame by frame instead of "being creative".

I've never edited a video. Can I still do this?

Yes. You give Claude your clip, paste the prompt, and give notes like "make the captions bigger". If you can write a message, you can do this.

What do I need to start?

Just Claude. Nothing else. Any normal Mac or Windows laptop that runs Claude works.

Every edit we teach comes with a skill file under the lesson video. Download it and give it to your Claude, and it knows that edit: the look, the captions, the timing. Then you just give it your footage. 36 skills inside right now.
