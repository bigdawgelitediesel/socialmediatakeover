Prompt B
Copy the edit of reference.mp4 onto my clip raw.mp4, 1:1. Same shots, timing, layout, fonts, colours, motion and sound. 1080x1920, 30 fps. Build it in code: every shot is an HTML/CSS/JS page that headless Chrome renders frame by frame (Playwright), ffmpeg for the audio and the final encode. No After Effects.

1. Split reference.mp4 into shots (every cut is a hard cut). For each shot write REF.md: what is on screen, frame by frame, with every number measured from the frames, never guessed: positions and sizes in px, fonts (match by x-height and letter shapes), colours sampled from the frame, when each word appears, how it moves and on what curve.
2. Transcribe raw.mp4 with word timestamps and line my sentences up with the reference shots. Cut only in real pauses. Never stretch the voice.
3. raw.mp4 is 16:9 with two speakers. For every speaker shot, crop 9:16 on the speaker with the same framing as the reference. One still crop per shot, no fake camera moves. The only move is the zoom-out on the first shot.
4. B-roll: for every b-roll shot find a free still of the same subject (Pexels, Unsplash) and frame it the same way. Never cut images out of the reference.
5. Build one shot at a time. Render it next to the reference frame for frame, write DIFF.md (every difference, worst first), fix, compare again. Move on only when the shot matches.
6. Graphics (name tags, pills, bars, chips, the glass tiles): rebuild them clean and sharp at full resolution.
7. Sound: the reference's whooshes, clicks and the glitch on the same frames, a quiet bed under the voice, master around -14 LUFS.
8. Finish with a side-by-side (reference left, mine right) and a per-frame motion check so nothing jitters. Don't ask me questions, show me the result.
Prompt A · your own clip

The same style on your footage

Prompt A
You are my video editor. Edit raw.mp4 (my podcast or talking clip, 16:9, 20-30 s) into a 1080x1920, 30 fps reel in the style below. Build every shot as an HTML/CSS/JS page that headless Chrome renders frame by frame (Playwright), ffmpeg for the audio and the final encode. No After Effects, no Premiere, no timeline editor.

First: transcribe raw.mp4 with word timestamps (Whisper), then write the beat plan: for every sentence, which shot it gets (speaker full screen, full-screen b-roll or split screen) and the exact second it starts. A b-roll or a split every 2-4 s, never more than 4 s on a talking head. Show me the plan before you render anything. What I talk about: [my topic, my offer].

STYLE (warm and editorial)
- Colours: rust #A24A24 and terracotta #C77136 for every accent, peach #ECA572 or cream #E1D1BB for one big word, warm white #FBF8F3 for captions, red #DD3C37 only for the "!" icons.
- Type: Inter for the captions (small lowercase lines, thin caps lines, heavy caps lines), Bebas Neue for the big accent words.
- Text zone: captions only between y 950 and 1410, at least 51 px from the left and 58 px from the right. Nothing in the top half on full-screen shots.
1. Speaker shots: the footage untouched, a black gradient over the bottom third. The first shot zooms out from 115 % to 100 % and flashes a white silhouette of me on frames 1, 3 and 5 with a glitch click.
2. Name tag on the first frame of every speaker: dark tag at 68 % opacity, white name, small role under it, a blue verified badge, slides in from the left.
3. Every caption word slides in from 29 px on the right with a circ.out ease. 1-3 lines per page and one accent per page: a rust pill, a Bebas word on a rust bar that wipes open from the left, or one big peach or cream word.
4. Full-screen b-roll: free stills that slide right to left from the cut, a rust gradient at the bottom, a fine scanline, the whole b-roll run stepped at about 14 fps. One caption with a red "!" icon and a rust pill spans the whole run and grows word by word.
5. Split screen: white canvas, two rounded panels (radius 22), me on the bottom, b-roll or the other speaker on top, the caption in the top panel.
6. Lists: when I name things, one white chip per item with a terracotta "+" circle, popping in from below as I say it.
7. A big feeling word ("anxiety"): cream and huge, with two frosted-glass "!" tiles tilted 12 degrees.

MOTION RULES
- Hard cuts only, no dissolves. Nothing animates out: everything leaves on the cut.
- Pills slide in 54 px from the side with a small spring. Bars wipe open with an expo ease.
- One accent per page, never two.

AUDIO A whoosh on every cut between speaker and b-roll, a double click on cuts inside a b-roll run, a quiet bed 15 dB under the voice, master around -14 LUFS.

QC Export a still of every shot. Check no text covers a mouth or leaves the text zone, check every caption against the word timestamps, then render final.mp4.

What makes it hit

Four things we learned

  1. 1
    Something new every 2-4 seconds.
    A b-roll, a split or a new caption page. Never more than 4 seconds on a talking head.
  2. 2
    Two warm accents.
    Rust and terracotta only, everything else warm white. The whole reel reads like one brand.
  3. 3
    One accent per page.
    A pill, a bar or one big word. Never two at once.
  4. 4
    Show the raw next to it.
    The before/after card is what makes people stop: they see exactly what Claude started from.
