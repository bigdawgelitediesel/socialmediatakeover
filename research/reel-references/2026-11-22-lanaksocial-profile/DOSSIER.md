## 3. The format, measured on the Maslow reel

- **Set:** one locked shot, sitting on a sofa against a wall with two posters, handheld lav-style mic held in her hand at chest height, natural window light. Never changes across 150 videos.
- **Hook:** spoken superlative with a number and a dare: "Here's how to make people disgustingly obsessed with your content using a psychology theory from 1943. Yes, I'm deadly serious." Burned title in a black pill top centre for the first 4 s ("How to make people obsessed with your content using psychology"). Credential inside the hook: "I've been doing this for 15 years."
- **On-screen grammar:** small monospace captions bottom third, every clause, with one or two words in yellow or green; black pill titles top centre for each beat ("DEAD. SERIOUS.", "BASIC PSYCHOLOGY", "HUMAN BEHAVIOUR", "WHAT DO I GET?", "NICHE DOWN" crossed out, "SHARE YOURSELF", "OVERSHARING", "YOUR STORY = YOUR PROOF"); a hand-drawn pyramid that fills one level at a time and then shrinks to a persistent progress badge top left; icon pills for list items (food, water, sleep; community, feel valued, reply back, more of that); little diagrams drawn in (a profile mockup with checkmarks, a grid of tiles, a dot matrix with a few lit for "a specific group"). New element every 2 to 4 s, nothing cuts.
- **Structure:** the frame (a borrowed model), "what this has to do with you," then one level at a time with the translation for creators, then the pitch, then the gate. 160 s, about 640 words, 150 to 160 wpm.
- **Ending:** the product and the comment word, spoken and burned ("COMMENT DISCOUNT").

## 4. What she teaches, from the fourteen transcripts (the ones that matter to Jacob)

1. **Maslow for creators:** certainty first (profile picture, name, bio, pinned post, covers tell people what they get), then familiarity (recurring formats, signature series, visual consistency; "it doesn't mean niche down, but something ties it together"), then belonging and esteem (community, reply to comments, share a little of yourself, stories, how you know what you know, content aimed at a specific group that makes them feel sane, clear opinions, pick sides), then self-actualisation (show people how to do things, before and after, hope). Top of the pyramid only works once they already trust you.
2. **The parasocial ladder (248K):** stranger ("is this for me?" call them out in the first seconds by identity, problem or desire), recogniser (mere exposure: same background, same line, signature series), follower ("a follow is a prediction," plant clues to what is coming), fan, superfan. Climb every rung.
3. **The why-you line (122K):** one subtle sentence of credential tucked into a sentence that is already doing a job, matched to the video. "Whenever I'm coaching content creators" or "as somebody who runs a seven figure business." Not a bio, not Pickles and Gary.
4. **"As a blank, this is my favourite blank" (314K):** the content idea that works in any niche. Identity as specific as possible, one recommendation, commit to it. "As someone who has moved 11 times, this is my favourite packing hack."
5. **Community, not followers (215K):** make people feel close (stories as the VIP room, reply to DMs with a voice note), give them a reason to care (three things you are passionate about, three that make you angry, within the niche, not rage bait), a reason to come back (an open loop in your life, a goal you are working toward), a way to feel involved.
6. **Six things every week for an obsessed audience (277K):** stories as backstage, one lazy badly lit video sometimes, an ongoing story, storytelling in small doses, clear opinions, replies.
7. **Copywriting (47K, 1.73 percent shares):** specific numbers instead of vague words ("315 weddings in four years"), frame it as a loss, give them a picture, add "because" to any ask, rhyme, and use the South Park rule: connect every sentence with "but" or "therefore," never "and then."
8. **When a video flops (734K):** under 50 views is a flag, check Account Status; under 300, look at follower vs non-follower and traffic source; do not delete; reshare to stories; repost a better cut after two weeks.
9. **Stop doing (120K):** posting without captions (most watch muted), posting without a text hook, hunting trending sounds ("won't rescue bad content"), over-editing ("you cannot polish a turd"), hashtags ("do nothing, it's not 2015"), commenting on strangers' posts for growth, deleting flops, racing to monetise.
10. **Selling (newest):** sell often, make it three taps to checkout, handle objections with visual proof.
