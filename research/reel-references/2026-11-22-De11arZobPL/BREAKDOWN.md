## Her list (transcript, in order)

1. Be original, authentic, go the extra mile ("you already know that though").
2. Post a reel or a carousel three to four times a week. Have a series.
3. Double down when something does well.
4. "Social media isn't a main street. There's not a limited amount of retail space." Someone else doing well does not take your room.
5. Refine your bio once a week until it feels perfected; the bio is for brand new people.
6. Make your highlights bingeable.
7. Use ManyChat at least once a week.
8. Use Repurpose.io to send everything to every platform.
9. Constantly open loops: when you answer one question, queue up another.
10. Be one day ahead: film in the gaps during the day, edit at night, post the next day.
11. If you are a good person with good intentions, let people see you; "had I been hiding behind photos or Canva graphics, that connection might not have happened. Let people make a judgment call on you."
12. Learn to construct good stories (Storyworthy, Story Wise).
13. Be generous: give away what everyone else charges for.
14. Do the unscalable thing for a season, then outsource.
15. A personal element that sets you apart ("cut your hair short").

## What to take for El Coyote

- **How she actually got it:** one utility video about a universal problem (where do your photos go when the hard drives die) that answered a question millions of people had, followed by a week of follow-ups answering the comments, then doubling down.
