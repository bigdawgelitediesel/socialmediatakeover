## The content (this is the one to keep)
"Most hooks tease secret information. Lead with the desired OUTCOME, then tie it to a relatable CHARACTER." Info hook: "Here are the three best algorithm hacks for Instagram" (views from people who never buy). Desire hook: "Here's how to go from 200 to 20,000 followers in the next six weeks." Step two: "Here's how Tony went from 220 to 20,000 followers in six weeks using this three-step system." Outcome plus character. He says five templates exist and gates them.
