## What it says, point by point

These are the five conditions for a post to be ELIGIBLE to be shown to people who do not follow you. They are a gate, not a ranking boost. Meeting them earns nothing; failing one keeps the post inside the follower graph.

1. No watermarks.
2. Add audio. He singles out photos and carousels.
3. Video is three minutes or less.
4. The content is original. Using someone else's material is allowed only if you significantly make it your own (his example in the captions: adding your own commentary).
5. The account is in good standing, checked under profile, Account Status.
