## Why it worked (50K likes, the second biggest of her year)

1. **A contrarian take said flat, in the first sentence, about the thing everyone in her feed is doing.** No "hey guys". The position is the hook. (Playbook/12: one contrarian take per episode.)
2. **Credential by implication, then stated in the caption.** 13 years as an editor is in the caption, not the first line; the first line is the opinion, the caption is the proof.
3. **The notebook is the whole B-roll budget.** One prop, one angle, her own hand. Eight inserts for the cost of a phone on a stand pointed at the desk, shot after the talking take. It gives the eye a change every 8 to 10 s without a single transition, which is the argument of the reel made by its own edit.
4. **Recognition, not news.** Everyone watching has felt the "Cocomelon for grown-ups" edit and never named it. The name is the share (rule 103).
5. **Us against the problem.** "I want this to be a corner of the internet where we refine our skills." Not creators versus AI, us versus the slot machine (rule 105).
6. **Series packaging and the open loop.** Serif title "Part One", "Follow for Part Two" on paper at the end. Same structure as rule 102, with a follow ask the house does not use.
7. **The generosity line.** "I'd rather give away all of my tips than see AI take over my algorithm." Giving away what others charge for (her own list, item 13).

## What transfers to El Coyote

- **The notebook insert is the daily talking head's B-roll.** A legal pad on the dash or the counter, his hand writing the point in Sharpie, shot top-down after the take with the phone on the dash mount or the Pocket 3 held overhead. Five to eight of them per episode, cut in on the noun, 2 to 4 s each. It is the paper tier of the kit done for free in camera, it needs no second person and no render, it keeps the cuts motivated, and it photographs the thought instead of illustrating it. Ranked questions, the three things before a diagnosis, the price next to the OEM price, the one line a customer said. Add to playbook/12 as the default insert.
- **Her thesis is the house thesis, now with a 50K receipt.** No grade, no transitions, no sound-effect bed, cuts on motion only, one lockup: the rules Jacob already set are the ones a 13-year editor is teaching as "think like an editor". When a comment or a trend says the reels need more motion, this reel is the answer.
- **Say the contrarian thing about your own lane.** Hers was AI editing. His candidates: "you don't need a lift kit", "the cheapest filter is the expensive one", "most of what gets bought at my counter was never needed", "the project truck is the one you already own". First sentence, flat, then the case.
- **Two questions as a recurring device.** "Am I being sold something? How can I differentiate?" is a two-line notebook page. Jacob's counter version: "Does it fix the cause or the symptom? Who told you that, and what do they sell?" A page he can reuse in several episodes.
- **The craft series is where the audience spends after utility earns it.** She grew on a universal utility video, then taught. For Jacob the order is the same: the relatable and trust episodes build the room, and the "how I film this, how I edit this" six-parter (already in playbook/12) is the spend once there are ten episodes out.
