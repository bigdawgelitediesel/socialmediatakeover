## Techniques to take

1. **Show the mechanism, in UI.** A fake timeline with clipping red chips vs clean green chips says "bad vs good sound" without a word. Our rule "show the mechanism" (from the Chandler study) now has a sound-design example: meters, waveforms, frame rulers.
2. **Before/After as a flip of the same card** (the timeline recolors in place) rather than two separate cards.
3. **Counters as proof**: "5/12 synced", "HITS 06", "FRAME 0547", "5,000,000+", "UNLOCKED 3/3". Every claim is a number that animates.
4. **Lock rows that unlock as the list progresses**: a stronger version of the progress HUD; the viewer wants to see the third lock open.
5. **A mascot that reacts** (walks onto the card, sits on the counter). Only worth it with a real El Coyote mark.
6. **Upload at 1440x2560.** Instagram re-encodes everything, but a higher-res source survives it slightly better. Our export is locked at 1080x1920; a 1440 test is cheap and worth one A/B.
7. **The three tools themselves fill our known gap** (UI click sounds and on-frame SFX placement were listed as open in the kit): Earshot (generates a sound in code), RealSFX (scans a timeline and places hits on the exact frame), Openverse API (5M CC sounds, searchable by an agent).
