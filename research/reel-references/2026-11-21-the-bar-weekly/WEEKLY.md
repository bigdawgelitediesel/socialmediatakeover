## 6. How this was pulled (repeat next week)
1. TikTok listing: `yt-dlp.exe --flat-playlist --print "%(id)s|%(upload_date)s|%(duration)s|%(view_count)s|%(like_count)s|%(title).80s" --playlist-end 8 https://www.tiktok.com/@justyn.ai`. Download with `--write-info-json -o "%(id)s.%(ext)s"`. Comments do not come through (`--write-comments` returned 0).
2. IG follower counts: `curl -A "facebookexternalhit/1.1" https://www.instagram.com/<handle>/` and read the og:description meta (followers, following, posts). Works for all three; the grid pks in that HTML are stale (months old), so do not trust them for "newest".
3. IG reel stats for a known shortcode: `yt-dlp --skip-download --print "%(upload_date)s|likes=%(like_count)s|comments=%(comment_count)s" https://www.instagram.com/reel/<code>/`. View counts come back NA.
4. Shortcode to date: base64 decode with the IG alphabet, shift right 23 bits, add 1314220021721 ms.
5. viashaan and chandler have no public TikTok or YouTube; new reels need a share link or a screenshot from Jacob. Once a link exists, yt-dlp downloads it and watch.py sheets it.
6. Dead ends this week (skip them): instagram web_profile_info (429), `?__a=1` (201 empty), imginn/picuki/pixwox (403), dumpor/greatfon/instanavigation (JS shells), Threads for chandler and justyn (login shell), Bing site: searches (nothing indexed).
7. watch.py: `python -I -W ignore VIDEO/tools/watch.py <file> --out <folder>/watch-<slug>` gives info.txt, scenes.csv, transcript.txt, words.json, sheet-NN.jpg, shots/.
