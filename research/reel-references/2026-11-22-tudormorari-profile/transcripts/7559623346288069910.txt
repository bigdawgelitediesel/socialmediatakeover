If you ever thought your job was bad, just remember. At least you weren't a worker on the Great Wall of China. It's 220 BC. China is torn apart. Seven kingdoms fighting, burning, falling, until one man rises above them all. Qin Shi Huang, the first emperor of a united China, he wants to rule forever, but he fears what's outside the empire even more. No man's, raiders, chaos from the north. So he makes a decision, build a wall, not a border, a monster, to keep the world out. So he rounds up his enemies and turns them into workers. Millions of workers are sent, farmers, prisoners, scholars, anyone who dared speak. They lay bricks under the burning sun, frozen winds, starvation, tens of thousands lost. Some are buried inside the wall, but the wall grows. And so does the empire. Dynasty after Dynasty rebuilds it, expands it, until it stretches over 21,000 kilometers.
