When Napoleon died, his doctor secretly cut off his finger during the autopsy and kept it. It was sold. Then sold again. Then it disappeared for 100 years. Then someone found it in a suitcase in New Jersey. A urologist bought it and kept it in his living room for 30 years. Then it went to auction. It sold for $100,000. Napoleon's finger is currently sitting in a private collection in America and nobody knows exactly where.
