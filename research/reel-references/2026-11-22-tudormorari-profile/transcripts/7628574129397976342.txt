When the ancient Egyptians mummified a body, the first thing they did was pull the brain out through the nose with a hook. Then they cut open the left side of the body and removed every organ except the heart. Everything else went into jars. Then they packed the body in salt for 40 days until all the moisture was completely gone. Then they wrapped it in over 4,000 square feet of linen. The whole process took 70 days. And it worked so well that we can still look at their faces 3,000 years later.
