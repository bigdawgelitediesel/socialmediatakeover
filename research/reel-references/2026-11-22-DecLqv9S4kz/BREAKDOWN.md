## Why it worked (25K likes on a reading list)

1. **It is a save, and it is a give.** A reading list of other people's best free work is the cheapest generous post there is; nobody is being sold anything and every item is a thing to come back to. Saves and sends to a friend who is "trying to do content" are the whole engine.
2. **Credibility borrowed, not claimed.** He names five bigger creators and quotes their view counts on the cards. The viewer trusts the list because the list trusts them.
3. **The card is the cut.** One take, zero cuts, six card changes. The eye gets a new object every 8 to 11 s and it is always the thing being talked about. This is the Mariah notebook rule in a different costume (DIESEL-HOUR 109): the change comes from an insert, not a cut.
4. **Numbered progress.** "1/5" to "5/5" above the card is the visible finish line; the viewer knows how far there is to go.
5. **Every item has a reason in one sentence.** "Start with that video." "If you want views and followers on YouTube." "If you want to learn the small nuances." The reason is the viewer's goal, not the video's content.
6. **The challenge context.** A man documenting 100K with an iPhone, posting a reading list of how he is learning. Ronny's fifth principle (a public current goal) is on display.

## What transfers to El Coyote

- **The curation reel is a free save magnet that is community-first by construction.** "Four videos every 6.7 owner should watch before buying anything", "the three channels that taught me more than the parts catalogue", "five Class 8 videos pickup guys should see". Name the creators, show the card with the real title and views, say in one sentence why it matters to the viewer. One take in the cab, the cards added in the edit (a YouTube card screenshot is a legal insert; it is not reposting their video). This also seeds the community: the people he credits see it.
- **The card is the insert.** For any list episode, the thing being talked about goes on screen as a card in the upper third for the length of the item, numbered. The kit's CardReel already does this; in CapCut it is a screenshot on a plate. No cut needed.
- **Say the reason in the viewer's words.** "If you want X, watch this" beats "this video is about X".
- **The head-of-media lesson.** The man who runs media for a seven-figure creator posts a handheld selfie with a lav mic, no cut, cards and captions. The polish is in the structure, not the picture.

## What does not transfer

- "Follow me for more content tips" spoken at the end. House rule stands: the cliffhanger names the next episode instead.
- The content-about-content niche. Jacob's version is diesel and the shop; the structure is what carries.
