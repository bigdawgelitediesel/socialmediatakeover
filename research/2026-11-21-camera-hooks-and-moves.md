## 3. What the Pocket 3 and the iPhone can do solo in November 2026

### Pocket 3 state of play

- Firmware: DJI's last listed release is v01.06.10.04 (Aug 26 2025). A Sept 30 2026 update is reported (Zelpio) adding a one-tap lossless 2x zoom (icon at bottom center before recording, not available in D-Log or HLG, fine in Normal 10-bit which is what we shoot), focus breathing correction (off by default, turn it on, it is made for product demos), and D-Log over webcam. Guaranteed updates end 2026-12-31: update in Mimo before then.
- ActiveTrack 6.0: double-tap the subject to start, tap outside the box or press the joystick to stop. Not supported in Low-Light, Panorama, Slow Motion, Timelapse, or Med-Tele mode. So 4K120 inserts and the 2x tele are untracked, locked-off shots. Face Auto-Detect is "designed for single-person vlogs: after the subject enters the frame, the camera automatically recognizes them and tracks them in the center." That sentence is the fender trick, straight from DJI.
- Tracking caveats from 2026 reviews: it loses lock on sudden direction changes and behind obstacles, drifts to the frame edge before correcting, and works best with the subject large in frame at walking speed. Gentle orbits, not spins.
- Motionlapse: intervals 0.5 to 60 s, durations 5 min to 5 h, up to 4 custom positions. Slow motion: 4K120, 2.7K120, 1080p120 and 240.
- Gimbal modes: Follow, Tilt Lock, FPV. Lock (full lock via Mimo or the settings) for walk-ins.

### The solo moves, with the recipe

1. **Fender set-and-walk-in.** Pocket 3 on the fender, screen flipped to selfie, Face Auto-Detect on, start recording on an empty bay or the hood. Walk in. The gimbal finds his face and centers him; he starts talking. Variation A (locked walk-in hook): gimbal in Lock so the empty frame holds and he enters it; the DJI forum version. Variation B (tracking walk-in): Face Auto-Detect so the camera pans to meet him; reads as a camera operator. Cut point: first word.
2. **ActiveTrack part-orbit.** Double-tap the part (turbo on the bench, the engine on the stand). Walk a half circle at walking speed, 1 to 2 m radius, Tilt Lock on so the horizon stays, subject big in frame. The gimbal keeps the part centered so the background slides behind it. That is parallax, the one thing the edit cannot fake. Three takes, different speeds. Do not talk during it; voice goes on in the edit.
3. **Self-tracking walk-around.** Pocket 3 on a tripod on the floor, ActiveTrack on Jacob, he walks around the camera carrying the part and talking. Same parallax, inverted. Works for the "let me show you the other side of the truck" beat.
4. **Gimbal tilt reveal.** Joystick tilt at slow speed from the tire contact patch up to the grille, or from the boots to the face. 2 to 3 s. Start the line when the tilt lands. Add a half step forward during the tilt for a push-tilt.
5. **Underslung low tracking.** Flip the Pocket 3 upside down and hold it at ankle height (the image flips). Walk along the frame rail, under the bumper, past the tires. Knees bent, heel-toe. Worm's eye.
6. **Motionlapse over an assembly.** Mini tripod on the bench corner, 2 to 4 positions set across the work area, 2 s interval, 30 to 60 min. Gives the "hours pass" beat for the build series in one clip.
7. **4K120 inserts.** Torque click, chips flying off the mill, rag pull, a wheel spinning down. Locked off, no tracking, manual white balance, shutter near 1/240 under good light (needs more light than the shop tubes give; open the bay door). 4x slow in the edit. Two seconds of real time per insert is plenty.
8. **2x tap for macro.** The 0.2 m minimum focus is the Pocket 3's known gotcha for hold-the-part shots. Set the camera 40 cm back, tap the 2x icon before rolling (Normal mode only), and the insert reads as macro without the focus hunt.
9. **FPV reach-in.** FPV mode, camera at chest height, walk to the engine and reach in with the free hand. Hands enter the frame. Viewer is in Jacob's body.
10. **Spin shot.** Mimo's 180-degree automatic pan. Use as a one-shot transition between two sides of the bay. Rarely.
11. **Ninja walk discipline.** For every walking shot: knees bent, land heel first, elbows in, slow exhale. The gimbal does not fix vertical bob. (HAL Gadget Lab Mar 2026, Doc Film Academy.)

### iPhone

- Lock AE/AF (long press) and Lock White Balance for anything that will be cut against Pocket 3 footage; the settings block in the playbook already says this.
- Action mode for walking b-roll when the Pocket 3 is on the fender doing the talking shot. It crops hard, so wide lens only.
- 4K120 is rear camera, iPhone 16 Pro and 17 Pro only. If Jacob's phone is a Pro, it is a second slow-motion camera; if not, 4K60 and let rife-ncnn do the 2x.
- 17 series front camera is square-sensor with Center Stage auto-framing: for a handheld selfie line to camera it keeps him framed when he turns to the truck. Minor.
- Cinematic mode for a tap rack focus between the part and his face. It records 4K30 and the focus map is editable after, so the pull can be re-timed in the Photos app.

### Osmo Mobile 7P and 8, worth it?

- Osmo Mobile 7P ($149): the Multifunctional Module tracks people in the native iPhone camera app, TikTok, Instagram Live and Blackmagic Camera, with gesture start and a fill light, plus it receives DJI Mic 2. Object tracking still needs Mimo. Lens switching in the native app confuses it.
- Osmo Mobile 8 ($149, launched Nov 2025): Apple DockKit tracking in the native camera and 200+ apps, unlimited 360-degree pan, ActiveTrack 7.0 in Mimo, 370 g, about 4 h with tracking and light on. Reviews (TechRadar, PetaPixel, Tom's Guide) are positive; fast sudden moves still break tracking.
- Verdict: not now. The Pocket 3 already does solo tracking with a better sensor and native 9:16, and the DJI Mic 2 already pairs to it. The only case for an OM8 is wanting tracked 4K120 on the iPhone, which no gimbal fixes (tracking and 120 fps do not coexist on the Pocket 3 either, and DockKit tracking in the native app is person-only). Revisit if the iPhone becomes the talking-head camera.

## 4. Physical transitions that read as expensive and cost nothing

All of these are shot, not rendered, so they satisfy the house rule "whip only when the camera moved." General rules from every 2026 source: plan the end of shot A and the start of shot B together; same lens, same frame rate, same shutter on both; exposure locked (phones drift in the dark frame); direction and speed matched across the join; shoot three takes at three speeds so the edit has choices; cut while the frame is blocked or blurred, never just before.

| Transition | Shot A ends | Shot B starts | Cut point | Timing rule | El Coyote use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hand wipe (SWIPE DOWN) | Gloved palm sweeps across and covers the lens | Palm already covering, pulls away in the new place | First fully covered frame | Same hand speed and direction both sides; optional 2-frame white flash at the join; music ducked 30 percent for 1 s | Bench to truck, counter to bay |
| Object wipe (the part) | The part passes close to the lens and fills the frame | Same or similar object clears the frame | Frame where the object fills the screen | Match object size and speed; soft whoosh at the cut | Turbo housing, a tire, the hood as it closes |
| Whip pan | Camera whips hard left (or right) off the subject | Whip in the same direction, landing on the new subject | Blurriest frame of each, butted together | About 4 frames of directional blur each side; land on the snare; shutter must match or the blur does not | Between shop locations, between two parts on the bench |
| Light block (push to dark) | Camera pushes into a dark area: under the truck, into the toolbox drawer, behind the engine | Emerges from dark somewhere else | During the dark frames | Lock exposure first or the dark frame brightens; 6 to 10 dark frames is enough | Under the F600 to under the Super Duty frame |
| Bay door | Door comes down (or Jacob walks through the doorframe so the jamb fills the frame) | Door going up on the new scene, or jamb clearing | Door fully closed / jamb full frame | Match the door speed; 6-frame crossfade of room tone, no music, sells the location change | Inside to outside, day to the next day |
| Walk past a post | Side-tracking past a lift post, a pillar, the truck's A-pillar | Emerging from a different post, same direction | Post fills the frame in each clip | Same walking speed, same side | Walk the length of the bay in two shots |
| Match cut on the part | End on a shape (round turbine wheel, round gauge, hex bolt) in a fixed screen position | Start on another object of the same shape in the same position | Shapes aligned, hard cut | Hold each 1.5 s; no blur needed; plan it before filming | Turbine wheel to tire, gauge face to headlight, old part to new part in the same hand |
| Match on action | Start a movement (hood opens, wrench turns, door swings) | Finish the same movement in the next place or time | Middle of the motion | Direction and speed consistent; voiceover runs across | Hood closes on the old engine, hood opens on the new one |
| Tilt to ceiling | Tilt up to the shop ceiling or the sky | Tilt down from the sky somewhere else | Frame showing only sky | 8-frame cross-dissolve allowed here, the one place a dissolve is honest | Shop to the machine shop, Cheyenne to the next state |
| Kick / boot | Boot swings at the floor-level lens and blocks it | Boot pulling away, or the next shot clean | Blocked frame | Cut on the thud | Walking to the truck |
| Snap or click | Jacob snaps, clicks the torque wrench, or taps the part | Same framing, new state | Exact frame of the sound transient | Zoom the waveform and cut on the peak; 3-frame scale bump 100 to 106 percent is optional | Before and after on the same part |
| Head drop | Look down at the end of A | Look up at the start of B | Face pointing down | Cut on the beat; 8 percent zoom on B | Same talking head, different day or location |
| Sound first (J-cut) | Picture of A continues | Audio of B leads by about 1 s | Picture change | Fade the lead-in over that second; already in the playbook's audio rules | Every location change, under any of the above |

Rules of use for this account: one physical transition per location change, not per cut. Keep the same device for the whole reel so it reads as a series (Norman uses the same film burn every time). The hand wipe and the object wipe are the two to default to; whip pan when the energy is up; light block and bay door for time passing; match cut when the two parts genuinely share a shape.

## 6. Add to the shooting brief (read this on the phone)

1. Every talking shot opens with a move. Pick one: walk-in, head-up, top-down drop, side sit, sit back, close turn out, lens block, reach-in. Never a static face on frame 1.
2. Text hook on frame 1, 3 to 7 words. The move plus the text is the hook; the spoken line lands by second 3.
3. Pocket 3 on the fender, Face Auto-Detect on, hit record, walk in. That is the default talking setup. For a locked walk-in hook, switch to Lock first.
4. One real move per reel that the edit cannot fake: part orbit on ActiveTrack, tilt reveal, pull-out reveal, or a foreground parallax pan. Three takes at three speeds.
5. Orbit the part, or talk. Not both in the same take.
6. Every location change gets a physical transition: hand wipe or object wipe by default, whip pan when the energy is up, light block or bay door for time passing. Same device all reel.
7. Transitions: same lens, same frame rate, exposure locked, same direction and speed both sides, cut on the covered or blurred frame.
8. Low angle rule stands: lens at knee height for wides, a foot off the floor for pushes, ankle height upside down for the frame rails.
9. Inserts: 4K120 locked off with the bay door open; 2x tap from 40 cm for macro. No tracking in either, so set it and leave it.
10. Walking shots: knees bent, heel first, elbows in, slow exhale.
11. Rag over the part before the camera rolls. The pull is the reveal and the cut.
12. Point at it, then cut to it. Hold the part to the lens, then pull back. Cheap tension.
13. One Motionlapse per session on the bench corner: 2 s interval, 30 to 60 min, 2 to 4 positions.
14. Update the Pocket 3 firmware in Mimo before 2026-12-31 and turn breathing correction on.
