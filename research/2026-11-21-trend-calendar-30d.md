## 3. PRI Show 2026 in detail

### What it is, confirmed

- **Performance Racing Industry Show, Thu Dec 10 to Sat Dec 12, 2026, Indiana Convention Center and Lucas Oil Stadium, Indianapolis.** Floor hours 9 am to 5 pm Thu and Fri, 9 am to 4 pm Sat. Exhibitor and media access only from 7 am Thu and 8 am Fri and Sat. More than 1,000 exhibiting companies, about 3,400 booths, 750,000 gross square feet, roughly 40,000 motorsports professionals. Trade-only; "qualified individuals in the motorsports industry can register directly," no PRI membership required. Registration $55 through Dec 9, $65 during the show. Show questions: Tiffanyh@performanceracing.com, 317-207-7347.
- **Race Industry Week begins Sun Dec 6**: Grand Opening Breakfast, Featured Products Showcase, PRI Ekarting Classic, Esports Gaming Arena, PRI Paddock, Full Throttle Social, Race Track Business Conference. Wed Dec 9 is the AERA Engine Professional conference at the ICC and the Media Center opens at 1 pm. Thu Dec 10 the Grand Opening Breakfast is followed at 10 am by the inaugural Motorsports Engineering Symposium (ICC rooms 237 to 239, free to registered attendees). Fri Dec 11 education includes "Using Tribology to Find Extra Horsepower" at 9 am. NextGen Connect runs all three days.
- **Diesel at PRI**: Diesel Motorsports (NADM) lists "PRI Show - Diesel Motorsports" at the ICC on Dec 10, 11 and 12. Diesel World's PRI 2025 recap said diesel "was integrated throughout the halls, standing alongside established gas performance brands" rather than in one diesel hall. Known diesel-adjacent 2025 booths: Hot Shot's Secret #3701 (which displayed Cleetus McFarland's Cummins-powered "Crazyhorse" Ford), SPE Motorsport #6043, PPE #7167. That Hot Shot's Secret / Cleetus arrangement is the pattern that matters for Rival: brands put creator builds in their booths, and that is the same mechanic that gets a truck onto the SEMA floor in 2027.
- **Media**: the Media Center is "exclusively for credentialed media," open Dec 9 1 to 5 pm, Dec 10 7 am to 5 pm, Dec 11 8 am to 5 pm, Dec 12 8 am to 4 pm; media register and pick up badges in the Media Center. No published creator or follower threshold was found. Credential questions: Juan Torres, 909-978-6722, juant@performanceracing.com. Press conference scheduling: Marissa Guajardo, 909-979-0782, marissag@sema.org.
- **How it gets covered**: trade outlets (Dragzine, EngineLabs, The Shop, Diesel World, PRI's own magazine) run new-product rundowns off the Featured Products Showcase and booth news; creators who go do booth walk-and-talks, "coolest engine on the floor" reels and engine-build shop visits around Indy. Creator coverage of PRI is thin compared with SEMA; it is a buyers' show, not a fan show. That is good for us: a 7.3-specific menu of PRI products has almost no competition.
