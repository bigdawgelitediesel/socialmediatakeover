# Talking-Head Topic Bank

**Jake wants to run talking-head heavy. That's correct** — PERSONAL is 14.80% on this
account vs 1.90% for BUILD, and it's ~5 minutes of work with no b-roll and no second person.

**Target: 4–5 talking heads a week**, with truck footage as the occasional change of pace,
not the backbone.

The only thing that kills a talking-head account is running out of things to say. Pull from
here.

---

## From his own truck (pickup — the primary lane)

- A decision he made and why → **CP4 disabled + FASS is the first one**
- Something he'd do differently
- What's next and what worries him about it
- What it actually cost vs what he expected
- A part he bought twice
- Something he almost bought and didn't
- What he'd tell himself before he started the build

## From the counter (semi — the variety slot, ~1 in 3)

- The part he orders most, and why it keeps failing
- **What comes back.** Returns encode what people get wrong — nobody else can see this data
- OEM vs aftermarket: when the cheap one is fine, when it isn't
- A price that would surprise a pickup guy
- What a day of downtime costs an owner-operator, vs the part that caused it
- The part on backorder and what people do instead
- What Class 8 guys do that pickup guys don't — and should

## Opinions he already holds

- Something everyone in the niche believes that he thinks is wrong
- Something people spend money on that they shouldn't
- Something people *won't* spend money on that they should
- A brand or category he'd never run, and why
- Advice he'd give someone buying their first diesel

## From the comments

- Answer one question, directly. **Fastest video he'll ever make**, and it's what made the
  15.46% post work.
- Respond to a disagreement — fairly, then defend the position

## From the hotshot years

- What actually breaks trailers
- What he'd tell someone getting into it
- The mistake that cost him

---

## The format all of these fit

```
0:00  open mid-thought — no greeting, no setup, no "so today I want to talk about"
0:03  the specific: the part, the number, the year, the mileage
0:10  the turn — what was surprising, what went wrong, what people get backwards
0:14  THE RULE — one line that's true for everyone, not just him
```

**15 seconds. One idea. End on the rule.**

The story earns the watch. **The rule earns the send** — and shares/reach at 0.045% is the
bottleneck.

## Rules that stay fixed

- Never "hey guys." Never "thanks for watching."
- Burned-in captions, always
- Face a window or the bay door — never backlit, never fluorescents alone
- Three takes of the opening line, then talk
- Caption written before filming — it outweighs the video
- Post 16:00–19:00
- One glance at skip rate after: **under 40% means the hook worked**
