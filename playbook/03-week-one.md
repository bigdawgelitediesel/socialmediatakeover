# Week One — the easy week · Wed Sep 16 – Mon Sep 21

**No intros. No "here's who I am." No truck walkaround.** Nobody cares who you are until
they care what you say.

Every video starts **mid-moment**, and your job becomes obvious from what you're saying —
not from you announcing it. By Monday the audience knows you're the parts guy because you
kept saying parts-guy things, which is the only version they'd believe anyway.

**Still zero research:** no part to pick, no prices to look up, no wrenching, no props.
All six film in **one 45-minute session.**

---

## WED 16 — 15s

> **"Guy came in today and asked for a part for 'the white one.'"**

Then the rest of it. What you asked him. What he said. How it ended.

No setup, no explanation of where you work — the story does that. Every diesel guy has been
on one side of that counter.

**Caption:** *He was serious. Year? "It's a Ford." What do you all do with this?*

---

## THU 17 — 15s

> **"You ever bought the same part twice in one week?"**

Yours or a customer's. No names, no plates.

**Specificity is the whole thing.** Not "parts are expensive." The exact one: what showed up
wrong, what it cost to find out, how long the truck sat.

**Caption:** end with *"Tell me I'm not the only one."*

---

## FRI 18 — 18s · the week's swing

> **"The most expensive thing in this building isn't a part."**

It's the guessing. The part cannon. Three parts thrown at a problem nobody diagnosed.

You don't need a price for this — you've watched it happen a hundred times.

```
0:00  the hook
0:04  what you mean — guys replacing parts instead of finding the problem
0:09  the pattern you see at the counter
0:14  "Diagnose it once. It's cheaper than being wrong three times."
```

**Caption:** *Watched it again today. Share this with whoever's about to throw a third part at it.*

This is a real opinion you hold, and it's the first thing this week that gets *sent*.

---

## SAT 19 — 12s · 90 seconds to make

Text on black, or straight to camera in the truck.

> **"Nobody's coming to fix it. That's why you learned."**

**Caption:** the line again, then *"Share this if you needed it today."*

---

## SUN 20 — 15s

> **"What are you running? I'll go first."**

Your truck, your mods, your miles. Then *"Your turn — drop it below."*

Costs nothing to answer, and every one of these guys wants to say what his truck does.
**The replies become week two's content.**

---

## MON 21 — 18s · leads into parts without announcing anything

> **"Three guys this month, same truck, same part. I'm starting to see a pattern."**

Don't name the part yet. Describe the *pattern* — same engine, same mileage range, same
failure. Then:

*"I'll show you what it is Wednesday."*

```
0:00  the hook
0:05  same engine, same mileage, same symptom
0:11  "I order the replacements, so I see it three times before you see it once."
0:15  "Wednesday."
```

**Caption:** *Same part, third time this month. Wednesday I'll show you which one.*

**That's the handoff — a cliffhanger, not an announcement.** You've now earned the parts
content by demonstrating you have it, and there's a specific reason to come back.

---

## Filming all six

**One 45-minute session.** Change one visible thing between videos — hat, jacket, move a few
feet. That's what keeps six videos from looking like one.

Three takes of each hook. Roll long, don't stop for stumbles.

- **12–18 seconds**
- Face the bay door or a window — never backlit, never fluorescents alone
- **Burned-in captions on everything**
- Never "hey guys." Never "thanks for watching."
- Caption written before you film — it outweighs the video
- Post 4–7pm
