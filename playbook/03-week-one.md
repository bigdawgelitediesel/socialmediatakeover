# Week One — six videos, shot-ready

Everything here is derived from this account's own data. The rules it obeys:

- **Skip rate under 40%** → 5× the shares, 3.4× the engagement, 3× the reach
- **Shares, not likes.** Your like ceiling is 215. Your share ceiling isn't.
- **Hooks open on something happening, never on a product**
- **15–20 seconds** until average watch time clears 8.5s
- **Caption is weighted above the video** (Mosseri, April 2025) — write it first, not last
- **Education is ≤1 post in 5.** It gets saved, not sent.

`[BRACKETS]` are blanks only you can fill. Everything else is written.

## MONDAY — Counter Warning · talking head · 18s

**Hook (frame 1, part already in your hand, no greeting):**
> "This part is `[$__]`. Not replacing it is `[$____]`."

**On-screen text, frame 1:** `$11 vs $9,000` — the two numbers, nothing else.

```
0:00  hook, part in hand
0:03  "Third guy this month. Same truck, same failure."
0:06  what it is, where it sits, what it does
0:10  what happens when it goes — the real number   [zoom punch here]
0:14  who's at risk: [YEAR] [ENGINE] over [MILEAGE]
0:18  "If you've got one, go look tonight."          [back to the part — loop]
```

**Caption:** lead with the searchable phrase, then the question.
> `[PART]` failure on `[ENGINE]` — what it actually costs
> I order these `[N]` times a month. Every one of them was preventable.
> How many miles on yours?

**Pick the part by:** whatever you reordered most this month. Not the most interesting one — the most *frequent* one. Frequency is the story.

## Before you post anything

- [ ] **Settings → Account → Account Status.** 30 seconds. Rules out recommendation-ineligibility.
- [ ] **Name field:** "Jake Dalton" → `Jake | Diesel Parts`. It's an indexed search field doing nothing right now.
- [ ] **Bio:** what the truck is, what the series is, when the next one lands. The last clause is the follow reason.
- [ ] **Pin your three best-ER posts** — "It may cost you nothing" (15.46%), "This should explain it" (10.7%), "Don't be afraid!" (7.7%). **Not** the 260k reel — it recruits the wrong viewer.
- [ ] **Employer social media policy.** Before the first counter video. Never a name, never a plate, never a face, never enough detail to identify a customer.

## Filming all six

**One session, ~75 minutes.** Three locations — counter, shop floor, driveway with Chief. Film 3–5 hook variants of each (costs 40 seconds, biggest lever you have). Change one visible thing per location: hat on/off, jacket, different part in hand.

**Then 10 minutes of b-roll** — 5-second shop shots. That becomes your standing library and kills the biggest time sink in editing.

**Edit in two sittings.** Cut every breath. Word-by-word captions with a color pop. Stopwatch audit: 5–7 visual changes per 10 seconds.
