# Week One — the easy week · Wed Sep 16 – Mon Sep 21

**Nothing here needs a part, a price, or a lookup.** No wrenching, no research, no props.
Six videos that are just you talking.

Two reasons this is the right week one:

1. **It's your best pillar.** PERSONAL content runs **14.80% engagement** on this account.
   BUILD runs 1.90%. You already know how to do the easy thing well.
2. **It sets up the parts content.** By Monday, people know who you are and that you're
   about to start telling them what breaks. Week two lands on an audience that's expecting
   it instead of cold.

All six can be filmed in **one 45-minute session**. Pocket 3 on the counter, ActiveTrack on.

---

## THU 17 — Your truck · 18s

> **"Everybody asks what I drive. Here she is."**

Walk around it. What it is, what you're doing to it, what it's for. **First person, not a spec sheet.** Why *you* wanted this truck.

End on: *"She's not done. That's kind of the point."*

**Caption:** 2011 F350. Named her Chief. Building her out one paycheck at a time.

---

## FRI 18 — Relatable · 15s

> **"You ever bought the same part twice in one week?"**

Then the story. Yours or a customer's — no names, no plates.

**Relatability comes from specificity.** Not "parts are expensive." The *exact* thing: the
one that showed up wrong, the one you ordered twice, the guy who wanted a part for a truck
he described only as "the white one."

**Caption:** end with *"Tell me I'm not the only one."*

---

## SAT 19 — Motivational · 12s · 90 seconds to make

Text on black, or straight to camera in the truck. **Zero production is the format.**

> **"Nobody's coming to fix it. That's why you learned."**

Aimed at one guy: works, tows, can't afford downtime.

**Caption:** the line again, then *"Share this if you needed it today."*

---

## SUN 20 — Ask · 15s

> **"What are you running? I'll go first."**

Your truck, your mods, your miles. Then: *"Your turn — drop it below."*

Costs the viewer nothing, and every one of these guys wants to say what his truck does.
**The comments become next week's content.**

**This is the whole point of the easy week.** It ends with a promise, and the promise is
the reason to follow.

## Filming all six

**One 45-minute session.** Change one visible thing between videos — hat on/off, jacket,
step to a different spot. That's what stops six videos looking like one video.

Three takes of each hook. Roll long, don't stop for stumbles.

**Rules that still apply:**
- 12–18 seconds
- Face the bay door or a window — never backlit, never under the fluorescents alone
- **Burned-in captions on everything.** Most people watch on mute.
- Never "hey guys." Never "thanks for watching."
- Write the caption before you film — it outweighs the video
- Post 4–7pm

## This week's only job

**Ship six.** Don't judge performance — you're building the habit and warming the audience.

The one number to glance at: **skip rate.** Under 40% means the hook worked.
