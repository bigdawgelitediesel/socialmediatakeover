# Gear & Shooting — DJI Osmo Pocket 3

Specs verified against DJI's official page, 2026-09-15.

## Why this camera solves the one-man problem

**ActiveTrack 6.0 with Face Auto-Detect.** Set it on the parts counter, walk away, talk.
The gimbal follows you. This is the single feature that removes the need for a second
person — which is the binding constraint on this whole operation.

**Native vertical.** It shoots true 9:16, not a cropped 16:9. No reframing in post.

## Settings — set these once, never touch them again

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Resolution** | **3K (9:16) — 1728×3072** | True vertical. Instagram delivers 1080×1920, so this leaves headroom for stabilization and reframing without softening. |
| **Frame rate** | **30fps** | Matches Instagram's delivery. 24 is filmic but stutters on fast pans in a shop. |
| **Slow motion** | 1080p @ 120 or 240fps | Only for a specific beat — a part dropping, a turbo spinning. Not as a default. |
| Codec | H.264 | Wider editor compatibility than HEVC. |

**Do not shoot 4K 16:9 and crop later.** You lose resolution and the gimbal's vertical
framing logic. Shoot vertical natively.

## The one spec that will bite you

**Minimum focus distance is 0.2 m (about 8 inches).**

Holding a part up to the lens is the core move of parts-counter content — and closer than
8 inches, it will not focus. Hold parts at roughly a forearm's length. If you need detail
tighter than that, use the 2× digital zoom at 4K from farther back instead of moving in.

## Audio — the real weak point in a shop

Three built-in mics, 48 kHz AAC. **They will lose to shop noise** — air tools, compressors,
lifts, a running engine.

- If you have the **Creator Combo**, the DJI Mic 2 transmitter pairs directly. Clip it on.
  Use it for every talking shot.
- If not, this is the one accessory worth buying. Everything else is adequate out of the box.
- Instagram is consumed sound-off by most viewers regardless, so **burn in captions on
  every talking clip.** Treat audio as a bonus, captions as mandatory.

## Practical notes

- **Battery: 166 min** (1080p/24, screen off). A full day of short clips is fine. Charges to
  80% in ~16 min with a 65W PD charger.
- **microSD: V30 minimum.** Max bitrate is 130 Mbps — a slow card drops frames. DJI
  recommends SanDisk Extreme Pro or Kingston Canvas Go! Plus.
- **Operating range 0° to 40°C.** A Wyoming winter is below that. Keep it inside your coat
  between shots; cold kills the battery before it kills the camera.
- **Guaranteed software updates end 2026/12/31.** Grab any firmware update before then.

## The shooting routine

**At the counter (most of your content):**
1. Pocket 3 on the counter, screen rotated vertical, ActiveTrack on.
2. Part in hand at forearm's length.
3. One take. Say the thing. Don't re-shoot for polish — re-shoot only if you fumbled the fact.

**Three clips per subject, nothing more:**
- **The part** — hold it up, 5 seconds.
- **The failure** — where it broke, what it looks like, 5 seconds.
- **The number** — what it costs, or what the downtime costs. 5 seconds.

That's a 15-second reel before you've edited anything.

**B-roll in the shop:** handheld, walk with it. The gimbal handles the walking. 5–10 second
clips, no narration — you'll lay audio over these.

**On the 6.7:** same rules, but you can wrench on camera here. Mount it on the fender or
have it track you from a toolbox.

## What not to do

- **No re-shooting for polish.** Instagram's stated 2026 direction favors raw and human over
  polished. A dirty bay and a real part beat a clean set.
- **No 16:9 anything.** Every clip vertical, always.
- **Don't film longer than you need.** 12–18 second reels means ~30 seconds of usable
  footage per clip. Long takes make the weekly edit take longer, which is what kills a
  one-man cadence.
