## The three brackets

| Bracket | What goes there |
|---|---|
| **[SIZE / MODEL]** | Exact tire and wheel, the size. Specificity *is* the credibility. |
| **[LOAD RATING / SIDEWALL / TREAD REASON]** | One technical reason, in his own words, from what he sees fail at the counter. |
| **[REAL DOWNSIDE]** | Road noise, price, wear rate, ride — whichever is true. One. |

**The downside line is the highest-value sentence in the script.** Sponsor content on this
account runs 1.36% ER against 14.80% on personal. The difference is that sponsor posts sound
like selling. A stated drawback is what makes a diesel audience believe the rest of it — and
it costs the brand nothing, because he still bought them and still runs them.

reason his version outperforms, not a cost.
