Primary tribe the 7.3 Powerstroke crowd (OBS and Super Duty halves at once), then OBS Ford, 1957 to 1960 Ford and medium-duty, restomod-diesel crossover, Wyoming and the Front Range. ## Offer (what a viewer or a partner gets)
- Viewer: a real one-man build of a 1959 Ford F600 on a 2005 to 2007 Super Duty frame with a 550 to 600 crank hp 7.3 Powerstroke on compound turbos, documented honestly to the SEMA Show, November 2 to 5, 2027 (the target), plus a 6.7 F350 built for reliable 600 hp, plus memes between the wrenching.
- Partner: a part that is the payoff of a real episode, spoken not burned in, a Collab invite on a reel built to be sent, a Story thank-you, a place on the truck at SEMA (exhibiting partners first, two 50 square inch marks max for non-exhibitors), content they can repost from a 12-file kit.
