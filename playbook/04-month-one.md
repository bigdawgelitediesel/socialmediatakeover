# Month One — 24 videos

Four weeks, six posts a week. **~6.5 hours a week all in**, batched into one filming session
and two edits.

The month has an arc. You are not repeating week one four times.

| Week | Job | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Establish** | Learn the formats. Get a baseline. Don't judge results yet. |
| **2** | **Credential** | Introduce the semi angle — the thing nobody else has. First collab asks. |
| **3** | **Escalate** | Controversial takes. The series pays off. Ask for shares explicitly. |
| **4** | **Compound** | Answer the comments weeks 1–3 generated. Review the numbers. Decide. |

**The spine repeats every week** so it's never a blank page:

| Day | Slot | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | **Counter Warning** — a part, a price, a consequence | ~0, part's in your hand |
| Tue | **Showing** — the part going on a truck | filmed at work |
| Wed | **The Rotating Slot** — Receipt / Three Things / Price Gap | the week's swing |
| Thu | **CHIEF Ep. N** | filmed whenever you touch the truck |
| Fri | **Motivational** — text on black or straight to camera | 90 seconds |
| Sat | **Ask** — I Need Your Help / Comment Reply | fastest video you'll shoot |

**Mix:** roughly 2 pickup : 1 semi. Pickup is the lane; the semi counter is the credential.

`[BRACKETS]` = facts only you have. Everything else is written.

**Pick the part by frequency, not interest.** Whatever you reordered most this month. The
frequency *is* the story.

# End of month — the review  ·  Sun Oct 11

Pull the numbers and answer three questions. **Not likes. Not follower count.**

| Metric | Start | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Median skip rate** | 48.2% | **under 40%** | the hook rule. drives everything else. |
| **Shares ÷ reach** | 0.045% | **0.15%+** | the real bottleneck. 0.3% is the destination. |
| Avg watch ÷ length | — | 50%+ on a 15s reel | earns you longer reels |
| Followers | 215 | 260–300 | a *result*, not a lever |

**Three questions:**
1. **Which three posts had the lowest skip rate?** Those hooks are your template. Make more of them.
2. **Which posts got shared at all?** Shares are rare enough to inspect one by one.
3. **What did people ask in the comments?** That's month two's content, already written.

**Do not change the plan mid-month.** Six posts a week for four weeks is 24 data points.
Fewer than that and you're reading noise.

# The honest expectation

**You will not go viral this month, and that is not the goal.**

You already went viral — 260,008 views, and it produced 6 shares and almost no followers.
That proved reach isn't the constraint.

What a good month looks like: **median skip rate under 40%, shares/reach past 0.1%, and
40–80 new followers who actually own diesels.** Unglamorous, and it compounds. The viral reel
didn't.

At 1,000 followers you unlock **Trial Reels** — testing hooks on non-followers with no risk
to your grid. That's the next real gate. Everything here is aimed at it.

# What kills this

Not bad content. These three:

1. **Going dark.** Your history: Jun 7 → Jul 13 → Aug 16 → Sep 2, then six dark days
   *during your best reach stretch of the month.* **Bank 2–3 finished reels at all times**
   so a bad week at the shop isn't a dark week on the account.
2. **Perfecting instead of shipping.** Minimum viable quality is **clear audio + captions +
   a hook**. Ship it. Don't color grade. If it's a choice between polishing one and posting
   two, post two.
3. **Judging week 1 by week 1.** The algorithm needs reps to learn what you are. So do you.
