Section 04

How I work with brands

What you get

  • A reason the part matters, from someone who orders it professionally.
  • One stated drawback in every product video. It's why the rest is believed.
  • Sponsorship disclosed out loud and in the first line of the caption, every time.
  • Native shooting — the truck, the shop, the part in hand. Not a desk and a box.
  • Post-campaign screenshots of the actual reach, watch time and hold rate.

What I won't do

  • Claim a part is good when I haven't run it.
  • Read a script that doesn't sound like me.
  • Hide that a post is sponsored.
  • Promote a competitor's category against an active partner.
