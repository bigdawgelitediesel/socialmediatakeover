A diesel has no spark plugs. Zero. So what lights the fuel?

Nothing. It lights itself. Here is how, in under a minute.

A spark plug lives in a gas engine. It jumps a spark across a tiny gap, at twenty thousand volts or more.

Every power stroke. No spark, no fire.

A diesel squeezes its air much harder. Seventeen and a half to one, on this seven three.

That heats the air to around a thousand degrees. Spray in fuel, and it lights itself.

But cold iron steals that heat.

The glow plug fixes that. It is a heater. The tip glows orange, around eighteen hundred degrees.

Once the engine is warm, it shuts off.

A spark plug works every second. A glow plug works a couple of minutes.

One lights the fire. The other warms the room.
