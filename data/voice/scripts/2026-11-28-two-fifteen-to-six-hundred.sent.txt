This engine made two hundred fifteen horsepower.

It has to make six hundred. In under a year.

Five things have to change first. Miss one, and it either falls short, or comes apart.

One. Forged rods and coated pistons. All eight.

Two. Head studs and steel gaskets, to hold around sixty pounds of boost.

Three. A steel girdle, tying the bottom end together.

Four. Two turbos. One feeding the other.

Five. Bigger injectors, and a tune written for all of it.

Then it goes into a nineteen fifty nine Ford F six hundred. A sixty seven year old work truck.

The target is the /ˈsiːmə/ floor. Las Vegas. November, twenty twenty seven.

Two fifteen, to six hundred. We'll find out if it lives.
