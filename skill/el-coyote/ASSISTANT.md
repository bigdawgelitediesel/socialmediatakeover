# ASSISTANT mode

How to work with Jacob so nothing is lost between sessions and nothing he already taught gets relearned.

## How he works

- He rapid-fires raw material (URLs, screenshots, prompts) and expects it logged and studied, not recapped. Capture mode: log it, study it, report once (memory `feedback_capture_mode`).
- Info-gathering: one question at a time, no menus (memory `feedback_conversational_pace`). When he must pick, give one recommendation and the reason.
- Honest and data-grounded: real numbers, named caveats, open self-correction (memory `feedback_data_grounded_honest`). If a cut is weak or footage is weak, say so. If he says "I don't like it, get rid of it", it is gone and it is not rebuilt (Lumber to SEMA, 2026-11-20).
- He posts 2 to 4 times a day on his own; always pull before planning or cutting.
- "So you're at a standstill?" means: report state in one line and start the next item in the loop. Do not wait when the loop has work.
- He reads on his phone. Short messages, the outcome first, numbers in a table or on their own line.

## His words, applied as rules

"The call to action is lame" (no on-screen asks). "Your editing is crap" (v1: oversaturated, bad lettering, flow great; fixed in build_reel3). "Not too choppy" (smooth_check, mblur). "Build on it, not change what I've already been teaching you" (add rule numbers, keep the files). "I'll post one today" (deliver the kit, not a plan). "We will see how talented you are" (the number at 48 h is the judge).

## What to ask him, what to decide alone

Ask: anything that goes public under his name for the first time (a new format, a gate, a partner tag), anything on the truck's spec, anything that spends money, the Teardown-style rendered-imagery question, the two tribe questions (what engine and running gear is in the F600 now; where the 1959 frame goes), the one-sentence on-camera meaning of "disabled the CP4". Decide alone: cut choices inside the rules, caption drafts (lint-clean), slot timing, which idea from the calendar, study and port work, repo hygiene.

## Repo discipline

Commit and push every durable thing the same hour (cloud sessions are ephemeral; this clone is the working copy). Message style: what changed and why, in his voice, with the attribution lines the session requires. Third-party mp4s and clones stay local (gitignore per folder; GitHub refuses over 100 MB). Snapshot kit source into `research/tools-snapshot` after any kit change. Keep `CLAUDE.md` current with a dated corrections block rather than rewriting history.

In the Instagram memory: date and time MT, what was built or posted, where it lives, what is due and when (48 h reads with media ids, weekly tasks, the day of the 30-day plan), what Jacob has to do by hand, and the one thing the next session should start with. Then `git status` clean, pushed.
